Preparing for a Blackstone assessment invitation

Start with the exact programme and assessment named in your invitation. Free practice can help you understand a format; it cannot tell you Blackstone’s scoring or predict an offer.

TraitGame is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pymetrics, Harver or Blackstone. These are original preparation exercises, not the provider’s assessment or scoring. Your real invitation is authoritative.

Does Blackstone use Pymetrics?

Blackstone’s official 2026 US Future Leaders Program listing included Pymetrics Games and a Pymetrics Digital Interview as application requirements. This is evidence for that particular programme and year, not every Blackstone job, country or recruitment cycle.

The listing’s 9 October 2026 application deadline has passed. This page is preparation guidance, not an open application notice.

Source: Blackstone Campus Careers — 2026 US Future Leaders Program. Check Blackstone’s careers website and your current invitation for up-to-date requirements.

Games and a digital interview are different steps

If your invitation names a game-based assessment, begin with the Pymetrics and Harver practice guide. If it names a digital interview, check the recording instructions and prepare clear examples from your own experience. Completing TraitGame does not complete either step of a real application.

Before you begin

  • Check your deadline and time zone in the actual invitation.
  • Confirm which assessment steps are required for your programme.
  • Use the provider’s device and accessibility guidance.
  • Do not assume a score from another website is Blackstone’s pass mark.
