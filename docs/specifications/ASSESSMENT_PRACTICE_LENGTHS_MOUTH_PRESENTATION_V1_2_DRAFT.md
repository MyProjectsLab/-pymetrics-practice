# Lengths Mouth presentation addendum V1.2 — working draft

**Status:** Agent-authored presentation proposal under the founder's gameplay-fidelity direction. It is **not frozen or implemented**. An independent source-fidelity and implementation-specification review must pass first.

## Scope and evidence

This addendum proposes original face/fixation artwork and a separate versioned save family for newly started standalone attempts. It leaves [Lengths Mouth V1.1](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_LENGTHS_MOUTH_SPECIFICATION_V1_1.md)'s 90 trials, 45/45 short/long counts, reward opportunities, 50¢ earned rewards, input, timing, event arithmetic, validity and numerical report definitions unchanged. The [source-to-design ledger](../qa/lengths-v12/PRESENTATION_PARITY_DRAFT.md) records observed differences outside this face/fixation scope. This is not a claim of complete JobTestPrep or current Pymetrics fidelity.

The founder's saved JobTestPrep video `private-research/Pymetric /Lengths /Lengths Game/lengts screen recording .mov` visibly shows a centered, filled teal circular face with large pale eyes and a pale straight mouth at 00:16 and 00:20. Those two frames show a small mouth-length difference. A black fixation cross separates faces at 00:38–00:41. The saved guide screenshots in `private-research/Pymetric /Lengths /Lengths Guide/` show the same visual family; its teaching examples and scored presentation must not be conflated. The founder also relayed Pietro's first-person report that the real game's distinction was subtle and faces appeared one at a time. The [Pymetrics patent, Example 15](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11030554B2/en) independently describes digital faces with minimally different short/long mouths, Left/Right input and possible money after a correct response. It does not establish current colors or pixels. A previously cited Reddit account was not independently retrievable and supplies no evidence for this proposal.

Native decoded recording frames are 2268 × 1704. In the sampled native frames, the face spans approximately 239 pixels, eyes approximately 55 pixels and fixation cross approximately 200 pixels. A scaled QuickTime display suggested a face near 200 display pixels, eyes near 46 and painted short/long mouth widths near 85/96; those are **display-scaled estimates**, not provider CSS dimensions or calibrated target sizes. The exact JobTestPrep colors and stimulus durations are not established. This addendum uses independent SVG geometry and an original darker teal. The previously cited Reddit account could not be independently retrieved and supplies no geometry or mechanics evidence.

## Prospective visual contract

Every newly armed V1.2 attempt requires immutable `presentationVersion: "lengths_mouth_presentation_v1_2"` in its input/visual profile. Existing V1.1 attempts without that field remain in their original raw shape; absence is interpreted as V1.1 **only** by the historical validator for review. No V1.1 journal, embedded arm profile, activity or report is upgraded, rewritten or rescored. The separate save-family transition is specified below.

Render one original SVG per scored face in the existing 240 × 240 CSS pixel task stage, with viewBox `0 0 120 120`. The circle is centered at `(60,60)` with radius `48`, has no dark outline and is filled. Eyes are circles centered at `(42,53)` and `(78,53)` with radius `11.5`. The mouth is one centered horizontal line at `y=84`, stroke width `3`, with round ends. Standard short/long **centerline** lengths are `38/43` viewBox units, or 76/86 CSS pixels at the standard stage size. Round caps add 6 CSS pixels to each **painted end-to-end width**, yielding 82/92 CSS pixels. These are original approximate choices; still images cannot prove equivalent difficulty. The separately labeled accommodation uses `34/56` viewBox centerlines (74/118 CSS-pixel painted widths) and is never equated with standard performance.

In ordinary dark-on-light mode, use original solid deep teal `#006153` for the circle and white `#FFFFFF` for eyes/mouth on a white task background; feature/body contrast is approximately 7.41:1. In light-on-dark mode, use a white `#FFFFFF` circle and `#111111` eyes/mouth on the existing `#111111` task background; feature/body contrast is approximately 18.88:1. Use no color, DOM text, label, sound, shadow or other cue that reveals short versus long during scored play. The guide shows both labeled versions together; scored play shows one unlabeled version at a time. Fixation is an original geometric black cross on white, centered in the stage, 160 CSS pixels across with 12 CSS-pixel arms; inverse mode reverses black/white. It occupies the frozen fixation interval and cannot reveal the next class. Keep the existing blank phase, accessible response controls and size/viewport gates.

The actual onset authority requires the expected SVG geometry/colors, all visible face components, connected unobscured stage and controls, same viewport, and confirmed frame before a `face_presented` event. Fixation confirmation checks the intended geometric cross rather than accepting a small text glyph. A malformed, visually hidden or covered stimulus invalidates the timed attempt before a new onset is claimed. The feedback, reward, checkpoint and report-authority rules remain those of V1.1 within the new family. The guide must show the same V1.2 face as scored play; short/long labels appear only while untimed.

## Version and save transition

The shipped V1.1 engine requires an exact legacy input-profile shape and rejects added keys. Keep its IndexedDB database `assessment-practice-lengths-mouth-v1-1`, root, raw profiles, event journals, reports and activity bytes under their original validator. The V1.2 app may open that database for **read-only ordinary legacy history/review** and for the **explicit legacy deletion actions below**. It never inserts a V1.2 attempt, normalizes absent fields or appends `report_viewed` activity merely because the new app opened an old report. That prospective read-only review policy differs from a V1.1 bundle's ordinary view-activity write; it does not bypass current-root authority before visible publication. Historical absence of a presentation field means V1.1 only in the historical validator; it is never admitted for a new V1.2 arm.

New V1.2 attempts use a separate IndexedDB database `assessment-practice-lengths-mouth-v1-2`, root identifier `lengths_mouth_storage_v1_2`, and the required exact presentation ID. The V1.2 parser rejects missing, malformed, extra or unknown presentation identifiers without deletion or conversion to V1.1. Its arm event retains the exact admitted V1.2 profile; reconstruction and report validation compare the stored bytes. Frozen form, timing, event and scoring rules remain, with a V1.2 replay validator for the new profile shape. Renderer selection comes from the validated save family, never a guessed default. A new report shows `lengths_mouth_presentation_v1_2` and the existing form/variant/scoring identifiers; it cannot claim cross-version equivalence or exact provider geometry.

The new guide starts V1.2 only. An already open old bundle may finish or start a V1.1 attempt in the old database before or after a new tab arms V1.2, including while a V1.1 attempt is live. Each remains a separate standalone run in its own database; neither root is passed to the other's validator. Family-specific V1.2 live/order Web Lock names and deletion-notification channel prevent accidental competition with V1.1. When intentionally reviewing or deleting legacy data, the new app instead uses the original V1.1 authority and ordering path, so it competes correctly with an older bundle. An old bundle's V1.1 Delete all or integrity-conflict reset affects only V1.1; V1.2 Delete all/reset affects only V1.2. Neither can erase, reject or publish the other's result.

The current app exposes a clearly labeled **V1.1 history** and, there, separate **Delete this V1.1 attempt** and **Delete all V1.1 data** actions even after all older tabs close. These are the only prospective writes to the legacy database. They reuse V1.1 installation identity, tombstones, revisions, ordered presentation/deletion boundary and authoritative readback. Individual deletion removes the chosen attempt and its report/activity without rewinding V1.1 form counters. Delete all replaces only the V1.1 installation, explicitly warns that its V1.1 exposure counters reset, and leaves V1.2 and legacy bar V1.0 untouched. The V1.0 history/deletion route remains available and separately labeled. A combined clear is outside this milestone; if added later it must explicitly clear every family and disclose any partial failure rather than claim both removed after one successful transaction.

Legacy deletion success is visible only after current V1.1-root confirmation. A rejected, committed-but-undelivered or retrying deletion remains uncertain until resolved; an old or new tab's late save/view callback cannot resurrect a deleted result. Publication and deletion compete on the same V1.1 ordered boundary in both orderings. A corrupt or unavailable legacy root is never passed to the V1.2 parser or automatically wiped. New-app V1.1 review may show verified old numbers but does not create new V1.1 view activity.

V1.2 standard/accommodation form counters begin at zero in the **new presentation family**, so an LM form can repeat across V1.1 and V1.2. Guide and history explicitly say that forms can repeat between presentation versions; never claim cross-version unseen status, combined exposure count or performance equivalence. Counters within each family retain its existing monotone rules. The separate database safely isolates old/new bundles but does **not** create one global ownership lock across those concurrently open versions. The runs cannot be joined into one mock or training chain; any future global exclusion or migration needs separate design and audit.

A failed or undelivered V1.2 arm does not become saved/eligible without exact current-root confirmation. Same-request retry uses the V1.2 store's idempotent admission path; canceled or replaced callbacks cannot publish after later action. Unknown profile data is handled non-destructively as review-only. No V1.1 bytes, including raw arm profile and exact report, are rewritten for presentation attribution.
