## 1. Gameplay and source parity

One attempt has exactly two mini-games with different partner identities, in order. Each starts at candidate 500 cents and partner 500 cents. An extra 500 cents is awarded to one participant, so the round total is always 1,500 cents. The recipient chooses the transfer. In mini-game 1 the recipient may **Give** 0–500 cents from the extra 500. In mini-game 2 the recipient may **Give or Take** up to 500 cents in 50-cent steps. No simulated cash changes hands. An allocation is shown after the choice, followed by one candidate 0–10 fairness rating, then the next partner or Test Complete. The completed report has one page per round, arithmetic and the candidate's rating.

## 2. Screens and controls

Exactly one stage from the following table owns candidate interaction. Every accepted button is single-use until its durable transition resolves; pointer and keyboard activation cannot double advance. “Next” and “Confirm” wording may use original short copy, but their distinct actions remain. Selection/dial movement does not commit money or fairness. Restart on Ready cancels its durable prearm ticket; Restart during an armed stage settles the old attempt and returns to a new unarmed Ready; Close on Instructions through Test Complete returns to Practice; both use the authority protocol below. Test Complete has no Restart. Report Try Again begins a fresh **unarmed** Ready; it does not allocate a form or show an award. Only a subsequent candidate Ready Next may arm a new attempt. A committed Completed attempt remains in history even if Restart/Close is requested after its terminal write, while navigation still honors the requested destination.

## 3. Original forms, arithmetic and report

Six immutable forms are defined in the companion JSON. Each form has exactly two distinct partner IDs and two bonus recipients. The first form is candidate/candidate to recreate the filmed path; others cover candidate/partner, partner/candidate and partner/partner. Partner actions are fixed original form data in 50-cent steps; no hidden adaptive or probabilistic behavior. A round's candidate action is chosen only during play, not stored in the form. Form ordinal advances only on confirmed arm, including a committed arm whose callback is lost. Replay gets the next form and reports whether a form has been seen before; clear-data semantics follow the product's local deletion disclosure.

For a round, `candidateStart = partnerStart = 500`, `bonus = 500`, `recipient ∈ {candidate,partner}`, and `signedGiveCents ∈ {−500,−450,…,0,…,450,500}` in round 2; round 1 allows `{0,50,…,500}` cents. Positive `signedGiveCents` means the **bonus recipient gives to the other person**. Negative means the recipient takes from the other person. Set `recipientBefore = 1000`, `otherBefore = 500`, `recipientFinal = recipientBefore − signedGiveCents`, `otherFinal = otherBefore + signedGiveCents`. Map final values to candidate/partner by recipient. These formulas conserve exactly 1,500 cents and never produce a negative balance. The candidate/player filmed path with +200 yields 800/700 in each round. Partner-winner round 1 +200 yields candidate 700, partner 800. Partner-winner round 2 −150 yields candidate 350, partner 1150. All arithmetic is integer cents and renders two decimals.

Report pages show only confirmed saved rounds, each round's partner identity, who got bonus, signed Give/Take amount, candidate and partner starts/finals, 0–10 fairness answer, and an equation in plain language. Include brief action-versus-rating comparison across the two saved rounds without scoring or judging consistency. A local history entry labels Completed, Incomplete, or Uncertain; only a valid two-round Completed record is a full result. No normalizing score, hiring interpretation, diagnosis, personality label, norm or percentile. “How the amounts were calculated” is arithmetic help; its source inspiration does not license proprietary strategy text.

## 4. Local events, authority and exposure

Version IDs are `money_exchange_2_spec_v1_0`, `forms_v1_0`, `event_v1_0`, `scoring_v1_0`, `storage_v1_0`. The journal records `attempt_armed`, per round `round_intent`, `bonus_presented`, `decision_committed` with signed cents and actor, `result_receipt` or conservative `possibly_revealed`, `fairness_committed`, `round_terminal`, then `completed` or `ended_incomplete(reason)`. Browser animation/slider preview, a selected but unconfirmed rating, and opened help are not scored actions. `report_opened` is idempotent view activity and cannot change money, rating or terminal bytes.

The authoritative local IndexedDB root contains installation identity, schema and root revision, form allocation counter/exposure history, attempts, per-round durable intent/decision/receipt/rating/terminal, report snapshots, and deletion tombstones. Each operation has one immutable request ID/payload and its applicable stage/route guards. Exit rechecks the latest same-lineage state rather than rejecting only because arm or terminal advanced the revision. Within one ordered write boundary shared with Delete all, validate installation, owner lease, attempt/round ID, form digest, expected stage/revision and tombstone; then commit exactly one event/state transition. A browser Web Lock or equivalent guards same-profile play ownership. Confirmed deletion/replacement permanently fences prior callbacks and view publication. Same-request readback resolves committed-but-undelivered writes; a conflicting retry cannot allocate another form or score another choice. Failed reads are unknown, never inferred absent. A bounded recovery path may offer manual retry, but it must not promote unknown to saved or permit new scored arm while settlement is unresolved. Corrupt present storage is blocked, not silently reset.

**Presentation contract:** the candidate may see a result only after a durable pre-reveal intent for its exact cents, sign, form, round and owner is confirmed; the decision must already be committed. A result can be labeled as saved after durable commit. `result_receipt` is a bounded **render receipt**, not proof a person read pixels: the exact glyphs and direction passed an owned-document/browser paint and visibility check under documented limits, current view/owner and no known occluder/mutation. External window coverage or human attention cannot be proven. A general mutable DOM/property scanner alone cannot prove this. If pixels may have painted before receipt commit or delivery, retain `possibly_revealed` monotonically so an exposed form cannot replay as novel. A hidden, transparent, altered, covered or unverified amount cannot earn receipt, but a known paint possibility must not be demoted to unseen. Receipt write and any withdrawal/restart/delete are ordered against the same root. A late callback may reconcile exact durable fact but cannot reopen play or steal a new view. The packet's browser acceptance gate must prove the chosen owned presentation topology on the actual route; feasibility prototypes alone are insufficient.

Round 2 cannot arm before round 1 fairness/terminal confirmation. An `ended_incomplete` attempt is reviewable but cannot become Completed by old callbacks. Restart/Close/lifecycle/owner loss invalidate the old view and initiate ordered play-capability withdrawal and settlement under a document-lifetime owner where possible; unresolved settlement blocks another scored arm and shows an Uncertain view label. Durable Uncertain status requires a committed transition and confirming readback; a held settlement read leaves durable status unknown. Pagehide after possible result paint preserves the strongest known exposure. Deletion overrides old navigation intent and result/report publication. Report/history opening uses an epoch and publication order shared with deletion, even without BroadcastChannel. Try Again opens a new unarmed Ready ticket only after the old Completed report is verified; it does not create an attempt. No in-round scored resume after reload; prior confirmed reports and incomplete review remain accessible.

### 4.1 Root and owner-controller contract

The companion [local authority and owner-controller contract](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_MONEY_EXCHANGE_2_AUTHORITY_V1_0_DRAFT.md) is normative. Its root reducer and owner controller jointly define input admission, route ownership, atomic arm, exit, terminal/report guards and publication. They are bounded executable reference evidence, not a complete browser game or paint proof.

There is no separate Ready seal. Arm atomically transfers durable ownership to the active attempt. A Close/Restart admitted by the current controller immediately fences the old view and later settles that same origin attempt even if arm committed first. Callback handlers may reconcile existing requests only; they cannot admit fresh inputs. Close during a pending Ready opening, Try Again or Restart binds the exact originating request and its successor, so either transaction order preserves the requested destination without allocating an extra form. A wrong owner, old screen token, old lineage or changed generation cannot control a later run.

Completed requires both authoritative round terminals, valid cents/fairness/receipt identities and exactly two derived same-attempt report pages. A terminal-first exit preserves those bytes; an exit-first terminal rejects. Unresolved readback shows checking/Retry and allows no saved-status claim or new scored arm. Try Again/Restart opens unarmed Ready; only a later candidate Ready Next allocates a form. An Incomplete review retains committed rounds/exposure and never fabricates a second report page. Publication requires the exact current root and local view epoch under deletion ordering.
