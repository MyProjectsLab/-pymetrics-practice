**Status:** Agent-authored candidate under the founder's full-product delegation, 2026-10-08. Requires separate numerical/specification audit before implementation conformance or freeze. V1.0 remains byte-identical and is not a close-fidelity implementation target.

## 1. Authority and evidence

This packet supersedes Digits V1.0 for new attempts. V1.0 remains the historical contract for any result bearing a V1.0 version. The Master Strategy governs product scope, original expression, accessibility, local data, mock and claim boundaries. V1.1 retains V1.0's exact browser observation, local-voice admission, input sanitization, frame watchdog, lifecycle invalidation, exclusive owner, versioned journal, transactional readback, tombstone/replacement, report-view activity and mock-between-games requirements **except where this packet expressly replaces a rule**. Implementations must reject mixed V1.0/V1.1 form, journal and scoring versions.

The founder's saved JobTestPrep video and guide show individually presented digits, ordered recall, a four-digit start, length +1 after a correct response and −1 after a mistake, and a stop after three **cumulative**, not necessarily consecutive, mistakes. The saved recording continues after mistakes and reports the highest correctly recalled length. These are JobTestPrep observations, not verified current employer or Harver mechanics. Original sequences, layout, copy, scoring and assets are required. Candidate wording says that this is Assessment Practice and is not an employer score or memory diagnosis.

## 2. Simple candidate journey

Library → short rule and one untimed example → one understanding question → optional presentation/input setup and input check → readiness → one continuous scored run → report/history. The default is visual with ordinary keyboard entry. The separately labeled auditory mode may be offered only with V1.0's full verified-local-voice, sample, audibility and callback admission; it is never silently substituted for visual. A nonvisual candidate who cannot use visual presentation may use qualified auditory play or untimed preparation. Entry accommodation, high contrast and reduced motion retain V1.0's labels and profile freeze. Setup and report controls preserve normal keyboard activation.

Show one digit at a time, then one labeled entry field and Submit. A visible keypad and Skip remain available as appropriate. During playback, no future digit or answer is in the DOM or accessibility text. During entry, the full target remains hidden. Display the cumulative mistake count unobtrusively. A submitted answer closes the trial once; then a brief neutral outcome states only Correct or Incorrect and the next length, never the target sequence. The next trial begins after an explicit Next action; Next has no idle deadline, but observed leaving invalidates and a reload without a captured terminal event is Incomplete. No scored six-trial blocks, mandatory breaks, running score, strategy pop-up or extra assessment appears. The report puts **maximum correctly recalled length** first, then the actual number of trials/correct/wrong and any supported descriptive breakdown. Do not show a pass score, trait or employer comparison.

The V1.0 library estimate “about 3–6 minutes plus untimed breaks” and all scored-block/break copy are superseded. V1.1 library and mock introduction say: “Time varies with your answers. Allow at least five minutes. A rare long run can take more than 20 minutes, plus any time you spend before Next.” The mock's overall duration must therefore be shown as an estimate with a variable Digits component, never a fixed promise. Next has no idle deadline; it is not a safe tab-away pause.

## 3. Adaptive scored contract

The first target has length **4**. On every closed trial, exact on-time Submit is Correct; every other closure, including Skip, timeout, partial or wrong-order answer, is Wrong. Maintain cumulative `wrongCount`, initially 0. After Correct, `nextLength = min(16, currentLength + 1)`; after Wrong, increment `wrongCount` and, if it is below 3, `nextLength = max(2, currentLength - 1)`. At the **third cumulative Wrong**, complete immediately: there is no next target. Correct responses between mistakes never reset `wrongCount`. There is no claim that the floor 2, ceiling 16 or safety limit below are JobTestPrep rules; they are explicit Assessment Practice boundaries.

A flawless run can otherwise continue indefinitely. To keep this beta bounded, after **40 closed trials** finish with a distinct `practice_limit_reached` reason. This is a complete valid Assessment Practice run, but the report states that the three-mistake ending was not reached. The maximum length is 16; a Correct at 16 is still Correct and the next target remains length 16 unless the safety limit was reached. The candidate is told before Start that a rare practice limit can end an exceptionally long run. This cap must not be described as a JobTestPrep feature.

No fixed total-trial denominator exists. For a completed run, `closed = correct + wrong`, `wrong` is 0–3, and `reason` is `three_mistakes` iff `wrong=3`, otherwise `practice_limit_reached` iff `closed=40`. For each transition before termination, the next target length equals the formula above. A timeout with a matching draft is still Wrong. The highest **presented** length is never substituted for maximum correctly recalled length.

## 4. Versioned sequence families

`ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_DIGITS_FORMS_V1_1.json` contains six visual and six auditory seed records, IDs `DV1`–`DV6` and `DA1`–`DA6`, with distinct mode-specific seeds and a SHA-256 over the compact `{version,generatorVersion,forms}` object. Accepted Start rotates the six seeds within the chosen mode as V1.0 specifies; committed arms consume one position, deletion does not refund it, and Delete all resets it. Repeats after rotation or Delete all are disclosed. No alternate-form equivalence is claimed.

The recorded compact-object SHA-256 is `5feb7ca195d7d7619c68904d79361b1a7e1ac2a25bab71a80bc4f437c1c48ee6`. `scripts/generate-digits-v11-forms.mjs` writes the seed records; `scripts/review-digits-v11-forms.mjs` independently checks their identity, digest and representative complete answer paths. A changed seed or generator needs a new forms version.

The constructor is deterministic and independent of answers except for the requested trial length. For trial index `i` (1–40) and length `L` (2–16), initialize unsigned 32-bit state to `seed XOR imul(i,0x9e3779b9) XOR imul(L,0x85ebca6b)`, replacing zero by `0x6d2b79f5`. Each next draw uses xorshift32 (`x ^= x << 13; x ^= x >>> 17; x ^= x << 5`, unsigned after the complete step) and emits `x % 10`. Candidate digits may repeat, including zero; no one-digit identity is used as a cue. Reject a candidate if it contains a four-digit arithmetic step-1 run ascending or descending, all its digits are identical, or it duplicates any earlier **presented** target in this attempt. Continue drawing from the same state and retry up to 1,000 candidate sequences; exhaustion invalidates before display. The prior presented targets and current `i,L` are journal facts, so re-generation is reproducible. This is a practical original sequence schedule, not a uniform or calibrated difficulty claim.

Before each trial, calculate and retain its target from the verified seed and previous presented targets. The target appears only one digit at a time in the candidate UI and in the local private journal; it is never transmitted to analytics. A completed or partially presented trial records its target and sequence-generator version so a report can reconcile it. A canceled pre-onset trial is not recorded as presented. On replay, verify every recorded target against the constructor using the prior presented sequence list. A manifest/hash or generator mismatch makes the historical attempt review-only.

## 5. Timing, input and authority

Retain V1.0's visual 700 ms confirmed digit display, 250 ms gap, 750 ms entry delay, 20-second standard or 35-second labeled extended entry, exact deadline exclusion, auditory callback timing, 2-second required-frame watchdog and severe timing/lifecycle invalidation. One `performance.now()` clock domain governs a live document. The default visual mode is the primary fidelity path; auditory results remain a different presentation condition. The first actual confirmed digit is the trial's exposure boundary.

Start requires an exclusive browser owner, committed arm and authoritative same-identity readback. After each `trial_closed`, save and verify the complete immutable known prefix **before** Next can start the next sequence. This trial boundary is untimed. A failed or undelivered checkpoint retains an in-tab review and one same-byte retry/readback action; it cannot claim saved status or start the next trial until current authority is verified. A definitive deletion or installation replacement retires the draft and removes retry. The Next admission uses the current installation, session, revision and owner in one ordered boundary with competing deletion; an earlier positive read alone cannot authorize display. Terminal completion/invalidation copies the immutable state before releasing the owner and commits/readbacks the result before any saved claim. All late callbacks, stale notifications and pending transactions are fenced as in V1.0.

The V1.0 six-trial protection interval is **replaced** by this V1.1 phase/authority table. One accepted Start begins protection for the whole live run, including pre-onset admission, answer closure, pending checkpoint, feedback and Next. A lack of idle timeout at Next does not end protection. The completed terminal event ends live protection; its immutable save may still be pending. Every state transition has one journal owner and a canceled callback cannot append to a successor state.

| Phase at observation | Hide, blur, freeze, page exit, owner/voice/profile loss or severe timing/layout failure | Deletion or replacement | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before committed arm/readback | Cancel Start; no scored journal or exposure | Cancel Start | A fresh Start may be requested. |
| Committed arm, accepted Start/Next, placeholder or pre-first-onset admission | Append `invalidated` to the in-memory known prefix; presented count stays at its observed value (zero if no digit onset) | Retire target; do not append into the deleted installation | Terminal same-byte save/readback if authority remains; otherwise in-tab review only. No late onset. |
| Digit presentation or entry | Append `invalidated` after all already confirmed digit observations | Retire target | Preserve factual partial exposure; suppress aggregate report. |
| Trial closed; checkpoint pending, failed, verified or feedback/Next waiting | Append `invalidated` after the closed trial; do not start another target | Retire target and remove retry | If a checkpoint delivery was pending, reconcile its exact bytes before a terminal invalid save; uncertain delivery stays in-tab without a saved claim. |
| Terminal event captured; terminal delivery pending | The timed result remains terminal; a lifecycle event cannot backdate an invalidation | Retire target and suppress saved copy | Read back exact terminal bytes or show an unsaved/uncertain draft with same-byte retry. |
| Reload without a captured terminal event | Durable known prefix is `Incomplete`; later display/exposure is unknown | Replacement still retires it | Never resume the live run; new Start uses the next seed. |

If a `trial_closed` and a lifecycle event race, order them by handler-observed monotonic time and event sequence. A closure already appended stays factual, then `invalidated` follows. If invalidation wins, the later closure is ignored. A browser cannot always deliver page-exit events; absent an observed event, reload uses the Incomplete rule rather than inventing an invalidation. A failed checkpoint is a recoverable storage state, not automatically a timing-invalid trial. A lost exclusive owner or changed voice/profile after arm is invalidating even at the Next screen. A definitive tombstone or replacement is retirement rather than a saved Invalid result.

An interrupted mock Digits run restarts at trial 1 with the next accepted seed; mock resume is only between games. The individual deletion and Delete all rules from V1.0 remain, including tombstones, installation replacement, no resurrection and immediate withdrawal of stale saved claims. Report/history admission revalidates the root before publishing numbers and binds that publication to the current revision/target.

## 6. Journal, scoring and report

Versions are `digits_spec_v1_1`, `digits_forms_v1_1`, `digits_constructor_v1_1`, `digits_event_v1_1`, `digits_scoring_v1_1`; storage may retain the V1.0 root schema only when it distinguishes all five identities and older records remain readable without reinterpretation. Events retain V1.0's ordered identity/time fields and use **`block: 0` for every V1.1 event**. Each `trial_closed` detail records integer `targetLength`, integer `wrongCount` after this closure, Boolean `exactCorrect`, and `nextLength`: an integer 2–16 for a nonterminal closure or **`null` for either terminal reason**. There are no `block_started`, `block_summary_ready` or `block_continued` events in V1.1. `trial_checkpoint_verified` is storage metadata, never a backdated timed event. The terminal event records `three_mistakes` or `practice_limit_reached` and the final wrong count. No event is appended after a terminal event except separate report-view activity. A mixed or contradictory journal is rejected, not repaired by a summary.

For each presented trial, reconcile the generated target, individual digit onsets/offsets or speech observations, one entry opening, at most one committed answer and one closure. `presented = closed + unresolvedPresented`; incomplete and invalid reviews show factual counts and suppress aggregate performance. A Valid report requires a terminal event, no invalidation and full journal/snapshot reconciliation. The headline is the greatest length among exact on-time Submits, or “No sequence recalled exactly.” Show actual correct/wrong counts and mistake-ending reason. Accuracy by length is `exact correct / actually closed opportunities at that length`, with the actual denominator, never the former fixed `out of 3`. Serial-position mismatch denominators are the number of closed targets containing that position; transposition/substitution/missing-suffix classification retains V1.0's literal string rule. **V1.1 displays no response-time summary**; raw handler observations and quality notes remain available for validity/review. Visual, auditory and accommodated runs are labeled separately. Reports and history contain no employer score, percentile, trait or guaranteed-improvement claim.

## 7. Required acceptance evidence

`ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_DIGITS_FIXTURES_V1_1.json` retains full answer/length arrays for five complete paths, including a third mistake on trial 40, plus exact-deadline, journal-mutation and authority-order cases. `scripts/review-digits-v11-forms.mjs` independently checks the numerical paths and deadline cases; mutation and authority cases are required later product/browser tests, not claims that a JSON review proves storage safety.

1. First target length 4; Correct gives 5; a Wrong gives 3 or 4 as appropriate; a Correct between first and second Wrong does not reset mistakes; third cumulative Wrong ends immediately.
2. Floor 2, ceiling 16 and 40-trial safety stop behave exactly; the safety stop is labeled and cannot masquerade as three mistakes.
3. A full-matching draft at the entry deadline is Wrong; an on-time exact Submit is Correct; Skip/partial/timeouts follow one closure and one next-length rule.
4. For at least six different answer paths, independently regenerate every target from seed/index/length and prior presented targets; tampered targets, lengths, wrong counts, next lengths, terminal reason and summaries reject.
5. A visual and an eligible local-auditory complete journey save, reload and reopen identical event/result bytes. Auditory callback evidence does not claim physical audibility.
6. Checkpoint failure, committed-but-undelivered callback, retry, deletion/Next in **both** orders, Delete all replacement, second-tab owner, cancellation and late callbacks preserve visible and durable authority without false saved copy.
7. Held input, exact deadline, visual frame watchdog, lifecycle/focus/layout failure, accessible entry, report focus, keyboard activation and 200% zoom receive bounded production-browser checks. Physical devices and screen readers remain separate qualification.
8. The frozen V1.0 source files remain unchanged. Historical V1.0 records are never rescored under V1.1. All new form/fixture hashes and source scope are recorded before one separate exact-SHA functional audit.
