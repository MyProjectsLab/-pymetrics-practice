**Status:** Founder-approved V1.0 specification packet, including the exact fixed forms and their uncalibrated difficulty limitation. The focused independent specification review **PASSED** the four implementability clarifications and seven added router fixtures in the five-file working packet. Balloons is approved for development, but implementation requires a separate instruction. Browser/device/accessibility qualification, deployment and release remain open. No Balloons code exists in the repository at this freeze.

## 1. Purpose and evidence boundary

Balloons is an original candidate-practice game about sequential choices under uncertain outcomes. Each round offers a choice: increase the balloon's possible value or secure its current value. Reports describe choices and outcomes in this Assessment Practice task. They do not diagnose a stable appetite for risk, assign a personality trait, predict workplace behaviour, or reproduce an employer's score.

The supplied JobTestPrep captures show 39 rounds, 13 each of three coloured categories, a five-cent increase per successful pump, Pump and Collect controls, a burst that forfeits the current balloon's uncollected value, three playable variants, and a basic money/collection report by category. The JobTestPrep guide states three probability patterns and discusses employer traits. These statements are reference material, not evidence of a current Pymetrics algorithm or permission to copy its sequences, art, wording, probabilities, scoring, or employer interpretations. The original Balloon Analogue Risk Task is a research precedent for pump/bank/burst mechanics, not validation of this implementation.

[Harver publicly describes](https://harver.com/gamified-assessments/) Pymetrics games as contributing many behavioural observations to broader candidate profiles that include decision-making and risk tolerance. The precise Balloons features, employer-visible fields, weights, thresholds, and hiring use for a particular invitation are unknown to Assessment Practice. The candidate's actual invitation is authoritative. Assessment Practice does not transmit this game's result to an employer.

## 2. Public Beta scope

The complete Balloons delivery includes Practice Mode, basic report, replay and local history, inclusion in the twelve-game mock, and Advanced Training: baseline, evidence card, one assigned drill, a new-sequence retest, and descriptive comparison. These can be built in bounded milestones under this approved packet after separate implementation instruction. No account, server-stored candidate result, proprietary employer match, composite trait score, or external analytics is part of this game.

## 3. Candidate journey, example, understanding and controls

`Library → guide → worked balloon example → two-question understanding check → input/accessibility check → readiness → 39 rounds → report → optional assigned drill → retest explanation → 39-round retest → comparison → history`.

The guide states that money is simulated, five cents is added only on a successful pump, a burst loses only this balloon's uncollected amount, earlier banked money remains, and balloon types may differ without revealing the next exact burst point. The worked unscored demo uses a separate demo balloon with hidden `T=6`: two successful pumps display 10 cents, then Collect banks 10 cents. Never show `T` to a candidate. Understanding question 1 asks what is lost after a burst following two successful pumps when earlier banked money is 15 cents; the correct answer is “the 10 cents on this balloon; the earlier 15 cents stays banked.” Question 2 asks whether observing prior balloons reveals the next exact burst pump; the correct answer is “no.” For question 1 show, in fixed order, “the 10 cents on this balloon; earlier 15 cents stays banked” (correct), “all 25 cents is lost”, and “nothing is lost”. For question 2 show “yes, the last burst fixes the next burst pump”, “no, earlier outcomes do not reveal the next exact burst pump” (correct), and “yes, each category always bursts on the same pump”. Announce the correct answer after submission and require both correct in one attempt. Retry the same two questions without an attempt limit; no scored form is assigned or exposed during this step.

The scored screen shows category text and a distinct stripe/dot/grid pattern for visible Types 1/2/3, current uncollected cents, previously banked cents, round `1..39`, and native **Pump** and **Collect** buttons. Type pattern never reveals the hidden risk profile. Buttons receive normal Tab focus and focused Space/Enter activation. Pointer and touch activate the same buttons. No single-letter shortcuts launch in this version. After a resolved round, disable Pump/Collect and focus a native **Next balloon** button; after Next, focus Pump on the newly confirmed round. After round 39, show a **View report** action instead of Next. Focus remains visible, including after a burst. Native keyboard activation is not intercepted or reassigned.

The native button's DOM `click` is the **only** game-action entry point, whether activation came from keyboard, pointer, touch or assistive technology. Pointer, touch and keyboard listeners may classify or suppress input but must never separately pump or collect. Do not use `navigator.userActivation`, `isTrusted`, or a browser-generated “activation token” as an action ID. On an eligible click the app allocates a monotonically increasing session action ID bound to session, round, control and committed state revision. One click/event object can enter the action handler once. Ignore a click for a stale round, disabled control, wrong owner, or pending write. The app commits the action and revised state atomically before re-enabling Pump/Collect; while a write is pending, show a quiet saving state and do not queue further gameplay clicks. A fresh intentional click after that commit is a new action, including a rapid second Pump. A failed write leaves controls paused and the last visible action explicitly unsaved. This is an untimed choice game, so storage latency does not alter a score.

Preserve normal first-press Space/Enter button activation. A capture-phase key-cycle guard suppresses **autorepeat** keydowns and their repeat-generated clicks for a held Space or Enter; it does not prevent the first native activation. Track the held physical key across focus changes until keyup. If focus changes before release, cancel that key cycle so release cannot activate a different control; blur/visibility interruption pauses controls and requires a fresh nonrepeat press after recovery. A click without a reliable preceding keyboard or pointer/touch classification is accepted as `unknown_or_assistive`, never rejected merely for that reason. Record the best supported per-action source (`keyboard`, `mouse`, `touch_or_pen`, `unknown_or_assistive`) and the action ID, without claiming to identify a user's assistive device. Mixing sources in this untimed task **does not itself invalidate** a result or comparison; the chosen controls reveal the same information and resolve the same actions. A mid-session setting change that changes information, rules or action meaning remains reviewable but ineligible for baseline/retest comparison and invalidates a mock game. Pump `T` bursts before adding value; Collect at zero pumps is permitted. There is no hard response timer. Decision latency is descriptive/QA only and unavailable across reload or pause. No silent comparison between different information or mechanic profiles is allowed.

**Guide copy:** “Pump to increase the amount you could collect. Each pump might burst this balloon. If it bursts, you lose only this balloon's uncollected amount; money collected earlier stays yours. Balloon types may behave differently, but earlier outcomes cannot tell you exactly when the next balloon will burst.”

## 4. Burst mathematics and fixed generator

Profiles A, B and C have threshold supports `1..12`, `1..24`, and `1..48`. For each precommitted threshold `T`, attempted pump `T` bursts before its five-cent increment. If `n<T` pumps succeed, the balloon holds `5n` cents. A voluntary collection after `n` successful pumps banks `5n` cents. A burst at `T` banks zero and loses `5(T−1)` uncollected cents. Survival through attempted pump 32 automatically banks 160 cents with outcome `cap_collect`; `T=32` bursts with 155 cents lost. Only C can reach the cap. For `0≤k<min(32,M)`, the model's unconditioned survival to collect after fixed `k` successful pumps is `(M−k)/M`; for `M≤k<32` it is zero. Its conditional burst chance on attempted pump `n`, after surviving `n−1`, is `1/(M−n+1)` for `1≤n≤M`, where `M` is the support maximum. These are model facts, not displayed probabilities or employer claims. Draws are uniform before whole-form rejection; accepted within-form thresholds are conditioned and dependent. No runtime burst draw occurs.

The fixed scored generator version is `balloons-scored-v1`. Candidate seed integers start at zero and increase by one after any rejected candidate. A candidate's UTF-8 seed prefix is `balloons-scored-v1:` followed by its integer as eight zero-padded decimal digits. For each draw, append `:` and an eight-digit lowercase hexadecimal counter starting at `00000000`; SHA-256 that UTF-8 string, interpret the digest as an unsigned big-endian 256-bit integer `x`, and advance the counter. For a draw of `0..n−1`, accept `x` only if `x < 2^256 − (2^256 mod n)`, then return `x mod n`; otherwise advance and retry. All draws in one candidate share its counter. Fisher–Yates shuffles move from the last index down to 1, swapping with one uniform index from 0 through that index.

For each of three 13-round blocks, start with four A, four B, four C and one extra profile indexed by `(blockIndex + candidateSeed) mod 3` in `(A,B,C)`, then Fisher–Yates shuffle. After the three block shuffles, draw thirteen thresholds for A, then B, then C, each as `1 + uniform(0..M−1)`, and assign each profile's thresholds in its order of occurrence. Reject a candidate if any identical-profile run exceeds three; any six adjacent profiles are `XYXYXY` for distinct `X,Y`; or any nine are `XYZXYZXYZ` for three distinct profiles. Thus “no fixed alternation” is testable. For each profile require at least one threshold in the inclusive bottom quarter `1..M/4`, at least one in the inclusive top quarter `(3M/4+1)..M`, and an integer threshold sum between `ceil(0.4×13M)` and `floor(0.6×13M)`, inclusive: A `63..93`, B `125..187`, C `250..374`. Reject duplicate playable signatures against already accepted forms; a signature is the ordered 39 pairs `(profile,min(T,33))`. The first nine accepted candidates become BL1–BL3, RP1–RP3, RT1–RT3, respectively. Candidate seeds are `1,3,15,16,20,23,24,33,34`.

[Fixed candidate manifests](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_BALLOONS_FIXED_MANIFESTS_V1_0.json) contain every round, profile, threshold, candidate seed, playable signature hash and manifest hash. SHA-256 of a manifest excludes only its `sha256` field and hashes UTF-8 JSON with lexically sorted object keys, compact separators and no ASCII escaping; the signature hash uses the same encoding of the ordered pair array. The manifest is immutable once played. A code implementation must load committed data, verify its hash and generator version, and never regenerate a played result. The [form review](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_BALLOONS_FORM_REVIEW_V1_0.md) reports independent structural checks and fixed-policy payout differences. These are founder-approved fixed manifests, not calibrated equivalent forms.

## 5. Form families, assignment, mapping and exposure

BL forms are eligible standalone baseline forms, RP forms are standalone replay and mock forms, and RT forms are retests only. Within each family assign the lowest-numbered unexposed, unreserved form. A first standalone Balloons scored start that can open a new chain uses BL; later standalone practice without a new chain uses RP. A user may explicitly start another chain only with the next unexposed BL. Mock always uses RP and cannot become an Advanced Training baseline. No played or previously exposed replay becomes a new baseline or new-sequence retest. When a family is exhausted, a scored replay or mock restart may use its lowest-numbered previously exposed form with an explicit **Previously used sequence** label; a new baseline or new-sequence retest is unavailable. A mock restart follows the Master Strategy's fresh-form-where-available rule and labels a repeat if no fresh RP remains.

Visible Type 1/2/3 maps to A/B/C when the assigned baseline form is **BL1**, B/C/A for **BL2**, and C/A/B for **BL3**. The baseline form number, not the count of completed or failed chains, determines this mapping. An assignment canceled with positive proof of no possible scored display may release its BL form; the next assignment of that form retains the same mapping but receives a new chain ID. Once a BL form is confirmed exposed or exposure-uncertain, it cannot be reassigned as unseen; the next eligible baseline uses the next BL form and its mapping even if no earlier baseline was valid. A chain's baseline, assigned drill and any retest retain the stored mapping. Independent RP attempts use the same three cyclic mappings by RP form number. Store the mapping explicitly with each session and chain; never infer it from a color or image. The visible type text and stripe/dot/grid pattern stay attached to Type 1/2/3. Changing this mapping, risk model, cap, payout or form generator creates a version boundary. All three families use independent threshold sequences; this is not form calibration.

An assignment first commits a durable `pending_exposure` record naming the form, session, lease owner and mapping while no scored element may mount. Immediately before allowing the first scored DOM mount or scheduling its presentation callback, recheck current ownership and any reservation expiry, then durably append `presentation_armed`. Only after that commit may the scored element mount. The first scored balloon is confirmed after two animation frames while the document is visible, its element has a nonzero rendered box, and its category and controls are present. At that boundary durably append `form_exposed` before enabling Pump/Collect. This two-frame rule is an operational confirmation boundary, not a claim to measure a physical display scanout.

Navigation, cancellation, hiding, lease loss or expiry **before** `presentation_armed` fences presentation callbacks and may return a pending form to unseen inventory only after the current owner verifies that no scored mount was scheduled or possible. A durable `presentation_armed` record, a possible scored mount, or a failed exposure write makes the form **exposure uncertain** if `form_exposed` has not committed; it cannot return to unseen inventory by timeout, navigation, reload or a missing callback. Fence all late callbacks. The same session may resume with that form only after integrity and ownership checks; until durable confirmation it cannot yield a valid new-sequence BL or RT result. A confirmed first display consumes the form even if an interruption immediately invalidates the attempt. If the 30-minute RT reservation expires before arming, release it and require the explanation again. If expiry occurs after arming but before confirmation, never substitute a form: a later confirmed display consumes this RT and invalidates the attempt; otherwise retain exposure-uncertain status. After confirmation, expiry cannot release the form. Guide, demo, quiz, setup and an unarmed reservation do not expose it. Later rounds use the same presentation confirmation but do not change exposure status. Individual-result deletion retains form exposure and chain tombstones; Delete all clears local exposure history with the Master Strategy's truthful warning that earlier use can no longer be known.

Opening the retest explanation after a durable valid drill reserves the lowest-numbered unexposed RT for 30 minutes, with durable issue and expiry wall times. **RT assignment is dynamic, not paired by number with BL:** any eligible chain may use any next-unused RT after an earlier chain's abandonment, reservation release or failed exposed RT. An unarmed exit releases a reservation; an armed, confirmed or uncertain RT follows the exposure rules above. An implausible wall-clock rollback or uncertain reservation ownership fails closed until reopened. One chain can complete exactly one durable valid RT; an exposed failed RT may resume only as the same session if §6 integrity allows, or consume another unexposed RT on a new attempt. An exposure-uncertain attempt cannot be called a valid new-sequence RT; an exhausted-family repeat cannot complete the chain. A chain cannot hold two live reservations. Cross-tab assignment and reservation use one installation owner/lease and durable compare-and-swap; stale owners cannot act. Store and report the **actual BL and RT form IDs**; do not assume BL1→RT1, BL2→RT2 or BL3→RT3.

## 6. Session validity, event boundaries, persistence and recovery

States are preparation, ready, active round, resolved round, completed, interrupted, invalid, report, drill and retest. A valid complete scored result has 39 confirmed presented and reconciled resolved rounds, a verified manifest, compatible settings, unique action sequence and durable journal/snapshot. Local storage failure leaves a clearly **unsaved** in-tab review but grants no durable baseline, drill or comparison eligibility. No network request participates in action resolution. Deletion and replacement-installation authority supersede late writes/callbacks.

Each immutable event records schema/specification/form/scoring/router versions, installation/session/chain IDs where applicable, stage, manifest ID/hash, mapping, round index, monotonic sequence number, **app-allocated action ID** and best-supported source if applicable, wall time, clock epoch ID, monotonic `performance.now()` within that epoch, exposure/ownership generation, pre-action and post-action pump/cent totals, outcome and validity code. A reload starts a new epoch; no latency is computed across epochs. `performance.timeOrigin` and wall time aid diagnostics but do not turn wall time into a trial clock. The ledger includes assignment/pending exposure, presentation armed, confirmed exposure, pre-display release or exposure uncertainty, round presented, each accepted pump attempt and its success/burst result, voluntary/cap collection, round resolution, interruption/recovery, setting/input change, completion, report view, router decision and evidence, drill start/completion, RT reservation/release/exposure/completion and deletion tombstones.

An accepted action first atomically commits its app-allocated ID, attempt and resulting state to the local journal and draft snapshot, then updates the visible value/outcome. A failed write pauses actions before any subsequent input; the in-tab UI may show the resolved action as unsaved but cannot advance durable eligibility. The exposure and action commit order above define replay authority. Round `n+1` is presented only after round `n` is durably resolved and Next is accepted. A duplicate event, stale revision/round, stale owner, action after burst/collect, or held-key repeat has no new event or pump. Completion/report eligibility follows durable reconciliation, never a UI counter alone.

Standalone practice, drills and retests may pause on hidden page, reload or browser interruption and resume the *same* threshold and last committed round/action only after journal/hash, exposure, lease, settings and snapshot integrity checks. Hidden time alone never bursts an untimed balloon. An uncertain half-committed action, unresolved first-display exposure or meaning-changing setting change is invalid for comparison; show review or restart with a fresh form if available. A source change among keyboard, pointer, touch and unclassified assistive activation does not itself invalidate this untimed task. After resume, suppress latency for the interrupted decision. An exposed RT resumes only as the same session, never as a fresh RT. An invalid drill restarts at its first round using its fixed drill inventory and gives no RT eligibility until a complete durable run.

**Mock exception, controlling authority:** Master Strategy §6 permits mock resume only between games. Interrupting Balloons during the mock invalidates that game's attempt; the mock resumes at the between-games boundary and restarts Balloons using a fresh RP form where available, or a labeled previously used RP if exhausted. Within-round recovery never advances a mock game to a valid result. Preserve the invalid attempt for review. Mock-wide resumed/multi-session labels and break policy remain those of the Master Strategy.

## 7. Scoring, censoring and basic report

All amounts are integer cents. Per round define `attempted_pumps`, `successful_pumps`, `voluntary_point` (only on voluntary Collect), `banked_cents`, `lost_cents`, and one outcome: `voluntary_collect`, `cap_collect`, or `burst`. A first-pump burst has attempted 1, successful 0, lost 0. A zero-pump Collect is voluntary with point 0 and banked 0. A cap has 32 attempts and 32 successes and is not voluntary. A burst at `T` has `T` attempts and `T−1` successes and is a censored lower bound on the candidate's intended attempt count; cap is censored at 32 successful pumps. Neither supplies a voluntary stopping point. Never reveal `T` after a voluntary or cap collection. Retain all observed successes, attempts, bursts, caps and losses in the journal.

A valid 39-round result must satisfy `voluntary_count + cap_count + burst_count = 39`, `collected_count = voluntary_count + cap_count`, `zero_pump_count ≤ voluntary_count`, `attempted_total = successful_total + burst_count`, and `5×successful_total = total_banked + uncollected_lost`. Each of the three categories must have 13 rounds, with the same equations within category. Total banked is the sum of voluntary/cap banked cents; lost is successful-pump value on bursts and was never deducted from earlier banked money. Denominators for collected/burst rates are all 39 overall and all 13 within category; state numerator and denominator if a rate is displayed. There is no composite score or overall risk score.

The category table always shows rounds, collected/burst counts, banked/lost cents, attempted/successful pumps, voluntary/zero-pump/cap counts. A full-category median voluntary point is shown only with at least five voluntary collections; otherwise display “not enough voluntary collections (n/5)”. Use the standard median (middle value, or mean of two middle values); retain half-pump medians exactly or display one decimal, without rounding before comparisons. Early is rounds 1–13, middle 14–26, late 27–39. Early/late category median comparison requires at least three voluntary collections in *each* of early and late for that category. Show each median and signed late-minus-early difference in pumps; otherwise say not enough observed voluntary collections and give the two counts. Post-burst descriptive text requires two disjoint §8 outcome windows; show the actual four points/outcomes for each. If unsupported, omit that statement. The first report's “one supported observation” falls back to the collected/burst count out of 39. A 39-round review shows visible category, accepted attempts/successes, voluntary/cap/burst outcome, banked and lost cents; hidden thresholds never appear in candidate-facing history.

All count and money comparisons use exact integers. Medians and IQRs use exact integers or halves; show `.5` when needed and no percentage change. Do not use collected-only mean pumps as a trait or improvement score. Total banked is one sampled outcome, not a skill or personality measure. **Report limitation:** “These are the choices and outcomes from this Assessment Practice sequence. A different sequence could produce a different amount. This result is not a personality score or a prediction of an employer's decision.”

## 8. Deterministic Advanced Training router

Use only a durable, valid, comparison-eligible BL baseline with 39 reconciled rounds. Evaluate all four candidate triggers in the priority order below, store exact numerators, denominators, exclusions, selected evidence and router version `balloons-router-v1`, then choose the first true route. A valid baseline with zero eligible voluntary points still routes to maintenance; invalid, missing, deleted or unsaved baseline gets ordinary replay and no personalized recommendation. Every recommendation gives observed numbers, a possible task advantage and trade-off, exercise purpose, and this task-only limitation. No employer or personality inference follows.

1. **Outcome reaction.** For each category, read its thirteen rounds in chronological same-category order. A candidate window is four *consecutive same-category observations* `voluntary_collect, burst, voluntary_collect, voluntary_collect`; a cap, extra burst or extra voluntary stop between these positions breaks that window. Let their voluntary points be `pre, post, rebound`. Require `pre−post ≥5` and `rebound−post ≥5` pumps. Sort all qualifying windows by ending global round, then starting global round, then profile A/B/C. Greedily select a window only if none of its four rounds is already selected. Two selected disjoint windows trigger. They may come from different categories. A burst's pump count is never substituted for `post`.
2. **Information use.** For each ordered high/low profile pair, count observed bursts in rounds 1–26; require `high−low ≥3`. In rounds 27–39 require at least three *voluntary* points in each member and `median(high)−median(low) ≥3` pumps. Evaluate all qualifying pairs. For the evidence card choose greatest early burst difference, then greatest late median difference, then alphabetical high/low profile. A burst is an observed outcome, not proof of a wrong decision.
3. **Category differentiation.** Require at least one ordered pair with early burst difference `≥2`. In the last thirteen rounds require three voluntary points per profile and `max(median A,B,C)−min(median A,B,C) ≤2` pumps. Select evidence pair by greatest early difference, then alphabetical high/low. A tied pair cannot change the route.
4. **Decision consistency.** Require at least eight voluntary points in one category. Sort those points; for even `n`, Q1 is the median of the lower `n/2` and Q3 of the upper `n/2`; for odd `n`, discard the center and use the medians of the remaining lower/upper halves. `IQR=Q3−Q1 ≥6` pumps triggers. Caps and bursts never enter the sorted set. If multiple categories qualify, choose largest IQR, then largest voluntary count, then alphabetical profile.
5. **Maintenance.** A valid baseline with no targeted trigger receives the 18-round mixed exercise with wording “No targeted threshold was reached in this session.” Do not convert an absent denominator into a personal conclusion.

The [25 legal fixtures](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_BALLOONS_ROUTER_FIXTURES_V1_0.json) and [fixture review](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_BALLOONS_ROUTER_FIXTURE_REVIEW_V1_0.md) include each primary route, equality boundaries, one-unit misses, absent denominators, overlapping outcome windows, caps excluded from IQR, pair/priority ties, exactly eight and zero voluntary points, fractional medians/IQR, competing consistency categories, nonzero burst loss and the attempted-pump-32 boundary. All 25 passed focused independent specification review. Ordering perturbation is **exploratory only**: independently permute the same 39 profile/threshold/action rows while preserving each profile's chronological action sequence and the 13-round block counts, then note whether each route changes. There is no invented statistical significance or automatic acceptance threshold; any material ordering sensitivity goes to independent review and founder disposition.

## 9. Fixed drills, chain eligibility and retest comparison

All five drill kinds have eighteen untimed rounds in three six-round blocks, the same five-cent increment and 32-successful-pump cap. Drill outcomes are descriptive, never baseline/retest scores. [Fixed drill inventories](ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_BALLOONS_FIXED_MANIFESTS_V1_0.json) are committed alongside the scored candidates. Generator `balloons-drill-v1` uses the same hash draws, counter and threshold assignment procedure as §4, with seed prefix `balloons-drill-v1:<kind>:<eight-digit seed>` for mixed drills or `balloons-drill-v1:consistency:<profile>:<eight-digit seed>` for single-profile variants. Each mixed block starts `[A,B,C,A,B,C]`, is Fisher–Yates shuffled, and must pass the same run/alternation rejections. The consistency drill has one profile in all eighteen rounds and is exempt from category-order checks. Require bottom/top-quarter coverage and a 40–60% threshold-sum band separately for each represented profile: for six draws A `29..43`, B `58..86`, C `116..172`; for eighteen draws A `87..129`, B `173..259`, C `346..518`. Try seeds from zero, reject failures, commit the first acceptance. Fixed accepted seeds: outcome 22, information 7, updating 5, consistency A/B/C 0, maintenance 1. A repeated drill reuses its fixed inventory and is labeled familiar; only one durable valid completion per chain grants first RT eligibility. No drill result is claimed to improve the baseline.

- **Outcome reaction:** Before play, show two unscored demo-only histories: three successful pumps then attempt 4 bursts with 15 cents lost (`T=4`), and three successful pumps then Collect banks 15 cents (hidden demo `T=9`). Play `D-outcome`, two rounds per profile in each block. After rounds 6 and 12, consider each completed round whose immediately next completed same-category round is a voluntary collection; show the first round's outcome and that next round's voluntary pump point, with at most the most recent such pair per profile. Do not skip an intervening same-category round or show a future choice. If no pair exists, say no matched transition yet. At 18 show completed-round counts and any supported transitions, without a required target.
- **Information use:** Play `D-information`, two per profile per block. After rounds 6 and 12 show prior observed collected and burst counts, with completed-round denominators by category, before asking the user to consider accumulated evidence. At 18 show the same counts for all eighteen.
- **Evidence updating:** Play `D-updating`, exactly six per profile, two per block. After rounds 6 and 12 and at 18 show observed voluntary, cap and burst counts with category denominators. Never reveal thresholds or a guaranteed safe pump number.
- **Decision consistency:** Select the §8 winning category; use `D-consistency-A/B/C` for its stored hidden profile, display its ordinary visible Type label, and play eighteen rounds of that type. At rounds 6, 12 and 18 show count, range and §8 IQR of voluntary points if at least two exist; otherwise state the count and suppress range/IQR. Show burst/cap counts separately. No ideal pump target appears.
- **Maintenance:** Play `D-maintenance`, two per profile per block. After rounds 6 and 12 and at 18 show only banked/lost cents and collected/burst counts for completed rounds; use no weakness label.

A valid drill has all eighteen reconciled resolved rounds, intact storage and stable profile; it grants RT eligibility regardless of money or behavior. An invalid drill restarts from round one, using the same drill inventory, without reserving RT. The baseline is one durable, valid, standalone BL result not already linked to a completed chain; the first valid completion locks that chain's baseline and assigned router version. A deleted/invalid/unsaved baseline or drill cannot grant or retain comparison eligibility. Once a chain has a valid RT, it is closed; further play starts a new chain only with an unexposed BL. Replay and mock results are never relabeled as baseline.

A valid RT uses its reserved unexposed RT manifest and the same 39-round mechanics. Compare only durable valid BL and RT sessions in the same chain with exactly matching specification, scoring, router, risk-profile model, type-to-profile mapping, cap, payout, visible identity/pattern profile and accessibility settings that change available information or rules. The **per-action input source is recorded but is not a compatibility key** for this untimed task; its action semantics must be identical. A version mismatch or deleted endpoint suppresses the comparison and shows the specific reason. For compatible sessions always show the actual baseline and RT form IDs and signed RT-minus-BL differences for total banked/lost cents and voluntary, cap, collected and burst counts (same denominator 39), plus per-type banked/lost and collected/burst counts (same denominator 13). Per-type median voluntary point is compared only when each session has at least five; early/late differences only when each relevant segment has at least three in both sessions. Suppress only the unsupported measure and show its observed denominators. Show no single improvement percent, causal drill attribution, personality label or employer outcome. State: “These are differences between two Assessment Practice sequences. Sequence difficulty and ordinary variation may also explain them.”
