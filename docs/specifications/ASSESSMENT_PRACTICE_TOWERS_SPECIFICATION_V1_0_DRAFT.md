## 1. Observable game and original form choice

The scored attempt is **one** three-tower puzzle. Five distinguishable rectangular disks must be rearranged to match a target shown above the live board. Only the top disk of a nonempty tower can be moved; any tower may receive it, with no size-order restriction. The candidate has up to **120,000 ms** and is encouraged to use few moves. The timer starts at confirmed live-board presentation. Matching the target finishes immediately; timeout ends as an incomplete review. A separate completion screen with Next precedes the report after a verified solve.

The saved video demonstrates one five-disk, one-puzzle run with a 3/0/2 initial distribution, a five-disk target on the left, a 120-second countdown, Undo/Clear All/Restart, and a 17-step/00:03-planning/00:55-solution report. It does not establish the range of other provider puzzles, exact undo step accounting or a timeout report. V1.0 keeps that visible structure and difficulty. Six fixed Assessment Practice forms relabel the same nine-move puzzle with original colors and patterns; this yields familiar versus unseen local practice without an extra level or invented difficulty progression. The source's exact colors/order are not copied as frozen provider values. Material differences from the observed structure require founder approval before freeze or code.

The three-disk question in the saved guide is **teaching only**. Use an original untimed three-disk example and one short rule check before Ready. Neither consumes a scored form, starts the timer nor enters the report. Candidate copy should be short and plainly describe the puzzle, two-stage click/tap/keyboard action, Undo, Clear All and the two-minute cap; do not copy JobTestPrep strategy text.

## 2. Form and puzzle invariants

- A board is three bottom-to-top arrays. Each of disk IDs 1–5 occurs exactly once. A target is the same five disks, all on the left tower; the other two are empty. Each form starts with three on the left, none in the middle and two on the right.
- Disk identity has an original color, pattern and spoken name; order and label, rather than color alone, identify it. Form JSON is immutable and addressed by `formId` plus SHA-256 of canonical bytes. Wrong or altered forms fail admission.
- The generator enumerates legal top-disk moves and breadth-first searches each form's complete finite board graph. All six fixtures independently require an optimum of **nine** placements; no larger-on-smaller rule is applied. A change to a form or optimal path creates a new version, never a silent overwrite.
- The lowest numbered retained-unexposed form is used first. Later uses are labeled familiar. Individual deletion releases an exposure only if no retained attempt of that form remains; Delete all clears the family. A reserved form that was definitely never presented can be released. A visibly presented or uncertain first board consumes exposure.

## 3. Candidate flow and precise controls

1. Guide → original example → rule check → Ready. Next obtains exclusive ownership, reserves the form durably and confirms the board is visibly painted before recording `board_onset(0)`. The target and live board are spatially separate, as in the captured game.
2. Click/tap the top disk of a nonempty source tower, then click/tap a destination tower. The chosen disk lifts visually. **While a source is pending, every board click/tap is a destination action**, including a hit on the top disk of an occupied tower; a different tower receives the lifted disk and a hit on the original tower cancels selection. To switch source, cancel first with the original tower, a visible Cancel selection control or Escape, then pick another top disk. No move is recorded until a valid different destination is activated. Keyboard controls expose three numbered source buttons and three destination buttons with visible focus: source buttons activate only while no selection is pending; destination buttons place or cancel by the same rule. Both source and destination are announced by name; a concise live state describes the bottom-to-top disks on all towers.
3. Every accepted placement appends one `move` with disk, source, destination, elapsed time, input source and action ID. An invalid, duplicate, synthetic or stale input makes no move. A held key cannot create repeated moves. After a valid placement the board updates only from the verified event; if its board equals the target, append `solved` atomically at that same elapsed time.
4. Undo reverses the most recent applied placement since the last Clear All, records `undo` and increases `undoCount`, while the timer continues. Clear All restores the original board, clears the undo stack, records `reset` and increases `resetCount`, while the timer continues. Neither refunds elapsed time or erases prior accepted placement events. These accounting rules are original because the saved recording does not show their effect on the provider report.
5. Restart ends the current attempt as review-only and starts a separate **familiar same-form** attempt from Ready after confirmation; the previous journal remains in history until deletion. The saved recording shows the control but not its exact result, so this is an explicit original recovery choice.
6. The active deadline is `120000` on a single monotonic clock. Native action capture strictly before it is eligible; a tie loses to expiry. A timely captured move remains eligible if its storage callback arrives late; expiry must await durable settlement/readback of that move. Hidden tab, pagehide, ownership loss, material wall/monotonic divergence or invalid display interrupts play to a review-only result. Do not resume a partially played scored board.
7. On verified solve, show an original completion screen without numbers and a single Next. Next records report-view activity and rechecks the exact durable result under the same deletion ordering boundary before showing numbers. The report includes a **Try Again** action matching the observed replay affordance. It returns to the guide/Ready path for a new, separately journaled attempt; the saved report remains in history. Normal form allocation chooses the lowest numbered retained-unexposed form (TW2 after a retained first TW1 solve), then a clearly labeled familiar form when all six are exposed. It does not add another puzzle to the prior attempt. Timeout/interruption has no solved claim and is available as incomplete review in local history. A failed or uncertain write never produces a saved claim until authoritative verification.

## 4. Journal, report and authority

The ordered journal is versioned `towers-events-v1.0-draft`. Every event carries attempt ID, form ID/hash, ownership generation, sequence, monotonic elapsed ms, wall timestamp and source. Event kinds are `board_onset`, `move`, `undo`, `reset`, `solved`, `expired`, `interrupted` and `exited`. A move carries `diskId`, `from`, `to` and unique `actionId`; the reducer independently checks the source top, destination, board conservation and monotonic time. Undo references the move it reverses; reset replays to the fixed start. Terminal `solved` is admitted only after exact target equality and elapsed `<120000`; `expired` is exactly 120000. Duplicate IDs, extra events after terminal, tampered hashes and impossible histories reject.

The immutable report is derived from this journal, not from live display counters:

- `status`: solved, incomplete timeout, interrupted, exited or invalid/review-only.
- `movesMade`: count of accepted placements, including placements later reversed or cleared. `optimalMoves`: independently verified form minimum (nine). `excessMoves = movesMade - optimalMoves` only when solved; otherwise show no excess-move comparison.
- `planningMs`: elapsed board onset to first accepted placement, or unavailable if none. `solutionMs`: elapsed board onset to solved, only when solved. On timeout show `timeUsedMs = 120000`, explicitly not “time to solve.” Preserve milliseconds and render `mm:ss` by floor of elapsed whole seconds.
- `undoCount`, `resetCount`, final board, form/version, input/device/accessibility context, interruption reason and limitations. One run's moves and timing are descriptive task observations; they are not intelligence, personality, employer-fit or hiring claims.

Use local IndexedDB with one root, versioned run/snapshot/exposure/activity records and request IDs. Re-read current root within every write transaction. One same-browser exclusive owner prevents two active scored boards. Start, deletion, replacement and visible publication share a cross-tab ordering boundary. Individual and Delete all remove journals, snapshots, reports, report-view activity and relevant exposure; stale callbacks and reads cannot restore deleted data. Notifications only accelerate withdrawal. Failure to acquire durable storage or ownership prevents scored start. Reload opens verified history, not midgame continuation.
