## 2. Exact candidate journey and mechanics

1. Individual entry shows an original guide, one coached example (`$10` start, send `$5`, triple to `$15`, return `$6.15`, final `$11.15/$8.85`) and a short comprehension check on tripling and the self-balance equation. Example/check never allocate a scored form. The separate Instructions screen has Start and Close; Ready has Next, Restart and Close. Ready Restart clears its UI epoch and returns to a fresh Ready without form allocation; Ready Close returns to the library. Neither effect was filmed, so both are original recovery semantics.
2. Ready Next opens a distinct **$10 starting-award** screen with only the candidate centered at `$10.00`; its Next opens a brief **connecting** state, where candidate and partner `$10.00/$0.00` first appear, then a **partner connected** screen. Connected Next opens a **partner/rules** explanation; its Next opens a **return-choice** explanation; that Next enters the transfer decision. Keep both participants and balances visible from connecting through final allocation, but not on fairness or Test Complete. The connecting state may be instantaneous under reduced motion, but the connected identity and each explanatory Next stage remain accessible. These pre-decision screens have no hidden form or scored event. The partner alias is an original neutral label, never a claim that another person is present.
3. Entry to the transfer decision durably reserves one form before showing the player at `$10.00`, the scripted partner at `$0.00`, and a labeled currency input. Any cent amount from 0 through 1,000 is accepted. Blank, nonfinite, negative, more than 1,000, and more than two-decimal input are rejected before commitment. A tentative amount may be changed; only one confirmed transfer is recorded. No transfer deadline or forced choice exists.
4. On confirmation, the chosen amount becomes immutable and `transfer_committed` is saved. Show a separate **tripled-pot/partner-response-ready** stage with candidate remainder, tripled partner pot and an explicit **Next** after any nonessential animation. Do not disclose the returned amount yet. That Next is the sole admission to the **final-allocation** stage: reveal returned amount, both final balances and a plain arithmetic explanation, then confirm their actual visible presentation before `return_presented` is recorded. The final-allocation stage has its own **Next**, disabled until that presentation and its authority are confirmed; only that Next opens fairness. Neither transition is automatic. The response is deterministic for that form; there is no live opponent, network request, adaptive policy or random draw during play. The exact hidden rate and future forms are not shown before the decision.
5. After a positive transfer, show one partner-fairness question with 11 separately labeled options **0–10** (0 unfair, 10 very fair); the answer is subjective and never graded. Selecting an option is **tentative**: the candidate may change it, Next stays disabled until one is selected, and selection alone writes no rating or terminal. The fairness screen's explicit **Next** commits the last selected rating once and requests the terminal save. At zero transfer, still show the fairness stage but explain that the partner had no opportunity to return money; its **Next** records `not_applicable` and requests the terminal save. This zero branch is an original necessary rule, not an observed provider fact. Restart, deletion or a held/stale Next before authoritative rating/terminal confirmation cannot synthesize a rating, completion or new screen.
6. Accepted fairness Next atomically commits the selected rating (or zero-transfer N/A) with the completed terminal snapshot. If confirmation is pending, show a checking/retry state, not Test Complete. Once saved, show a separate **Test Complete** holding screen with **Next and Close only**. Next authoritatively opens the saved descriptive report. History can reopen the report directly; reload from the holding screen returns to library/history. If deleted before Next, withdraw the holding screen. Report Try Again returns to the prearm journey; a new form is allocated only at its later accepted arm. Exiting before completion is Incomplete; reloading never resumes a live exchange.
7. A visible **Restart** control exists on Ready and throughout active play from award through fairness; it is absent from saved Test Complete, report and help. Before form arm, Restart returns to a fresh Ready with no form exposure. After form arm but before saved completion, Restart terminates the old attempt as Incomplete (or retains a visibly labeled uncertain draft while terminal delivery is unresolved); it never reuses its form or silently discards a possibly shown return. Restart first cancels the old UI epoch and is serialized with pending write, frame and deletion callbacks. A pending terminal that already committed remains Completed. The film shows control presence, not its effect; these are original recovery semantics.
8. A labeled **Close** action is available on Instructions, Ready, award through Test Complete, and arithmetic help. Before arm it returns to the library without form exposure; during an armed unfinished attempt it settles that attempt as Incomplete (or leaves a labeled uncertain draft pending exact authority); on saved Test Complete or help it retains the completed result. The report has an ordinary exit to the library as original navigation; its source header is partly occluded. Close never turns an uncertain terminal into a saved one. Next-to-report, Restart and Close cannot race into two owned views or allocate two forms. Source Close effects were not filmed.

The six original forms are `ME1-1` through `ME1-6`, each with one hidden `returnBps` in `[4100, 2000, 6500, 0, 8000, 3500]` respectively. A form is allocated in a durable installation-local rotation and is not chosen in response to the candidate's transfer. For a positive transfer, `potCents = 3 × sentCents` and `returnedCents = floor((potCents × returnBps + 5000) / 10000)` (nearest cent, half up). Then `selfCents = 1000 − sentCents + returnedCents`, `partnerCents = potCents − returnedCents`; `selfCents + partnerCents = 1000 + 2 × sentCents`. For zero transfer, pot and return are zero and partner behavior is unobserved regardless of the form's hidden rate. Never reveal or interpret the rate as a real person's intent. Form differences are not calibrated as equivalent difficulty.

The form counter increments once per accepted final pre-decision arm, including an attempt later interrupted. Cancellation before arm commit and a definitively failed transaction consume no form; cancellation or lost delivery after arm commit consumes one form and settles the old attempt. After six accepted arms, rotation repeats with a visible familiar-form label; Delete all replaces the installation and resets exposure. Individual deletion retains the counter and a tombstone. Standalone and mock use the same forms and storage authority. Future provider behavior, fair answers and employer scoring remain unknown.

### Observable screen and control contract

The gameplay screen table is `gameplay.screens` in the generated fixture. Its source evidence, visible content, controls, `admitWhen` and `publishNextWhen` fields are normative. `positiveScreenPath` and `zeroScreenPath` preserve the same observed screen sequence; the zero branch labels fairness N/A. A browser implementation may show checking/retry/review overlays between these screens, but those overlays add no scored round or source claim. A control is admitted only for the current owned view; its next screen is published only after the listed authority and actual presentation condition. The following are the source-backed stage boundaries; the fixture spells out all fifteen screens.

| Sequence | Visible action | Admission and publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instructions → Ready → award → connecting → connected → partner rules → return explanation | Start, then distinct Next actions; connecting advances automatically | Current view/epoch; award shows one centered candidate, connecting introduces the pair; Restart returns to Ready and Close leaves before form arm |
| Return explanation → transfer | Next arms the form | No transfer field or form claim before the exact arm transaction is confirmed; repeated Next cannot allocate again |
| Transfer → transfer busy → response ready | Eligible amount then Next; busy advances automatically | Valid cents and one immutable transfer request; do not reveal a return before transfer confirmation |
| Response ready → final allocation | Separate Next | Reveal the tripled pot response and final balances under the owned view, then capture actual presentation before committing `return_presented` |
| Final allocation → fairness | Separate Next | Enabled only after both captured presentation and exact committed return event, for positive and zero transfer |
| Fairness → Test Complete → report | Tentative 0–10 selection (or zero N/A), then separate Next at each boundary | Fairness Next atomically saves rating/N/A and terminal; Test Complete appears only after confirmation; its Next opens the current saved report |
| Report ↔ arithmetic help | Original help and Back; Try Again from report | Help returns to the same saved report; replay starts a new prearm journey |

Ready through fairness display Restart. Instructions, Ready, active stages, Test Complete and arithmetic help display Close. Test Complete has Next and Close, never Restart. Pending work disables duplicate scored Next; pending Restart and Close remain available as withdrawal requests, subject to verified settlement. Report has Try Again, arithmetic help and ordinary exit. These screen controls are separate from the local authority outcomes below: a requested Restart must still target Ready even if the old attempt is ultimately saved Completed.

### Included local authority protocol

The `authority` object in the generated fixture is the exact V1.0 implementation contract. Its dimensions are independent: immutable operation/request, delivery status, durable fact, play capability, navigation intent and visual exposure. Do not infer one from another. In particular, a confirmed Completed terminal does not replace a pending Restart intent with a Test Complete destination; the completed report remains retained and Restart proceeds to Ready. An Incomplete terminal does the same. Close proceeds to library. A deletion/replacement fact overrides every older navigation intent and publication.

The `outcomeRows` and mechanically expanded `routeCases` define all seven authoritative outcomes × four navigation intents. `continue` follows a current confirmed gameplay fact; `restart` always targets Ready after exact settlement unless deletion, invalidity or unresolved authority blocks it; `close` leaves for library while a document-lifetime settlement owner keeps the obligation; `technical` opens a review or blocked recovery. An unknown result blocks scored arm, retains its target Ready, and shows checking or Retry rather than a saved claim. A corrupt root blocks instead of resetting. A nonterminal confirmed operation must settle Incomplete before Restart/Close. The local run's play capability is withdrawn permanently before any Restart, Close, lifecycle or owner-loss settlement; late callbacks cannot restore it. Saved Completed and Incomplete reports remain versioned and accessible from history after the requested exit.

For each arm, transfer, return and terminal operation, `cancellationCases` expands Restart/Close/technical × fence-first/commit-first × immediate/held/never-delivered callbacks; terminal also expands positive/zero payloads. Each row records the exact journal prefix, form consumption, exposure, terminal fact and requested destination. Fencing precedes any replacement arm. An arm that committed consumes one form even if its callback was lost; one transactionally fenced before commit consumes none. An acknowledged transfer cannot be sent again. A committed terminal stays Completed even after withdrawal. Retry/readback use the same immutable request ID and payload. Every read/check has a 2,500 ms delivery limit, at most one automatic retry within 900 ms, then a visible manual same-request Retry. Timeout is unknown, not abort. A final failed read remains unknown. A callback after view change can update only the verified durable fact, never the old view.

The `returnExposurePoints` and `returnExposureCases` specify the critical reveal race. For one retained attempt, exposure is monotone: `not_revealed < possibly_revealed < captured_visible`. Merge new evidence by maximum only for the same attempt and installation; deletion removes that attempt rather than downgrading it. Before any return pixels, exposure is not revealed. Once pixels may have painted but before capture, exposure is possibly revealed. **Every admitted return write follows actual confirmed capture**, so exposure is `captured_visible` whether that write commits or loses to a Restart/Close/technical fence. Restart/Close permanently withdraws play, retains the consumed form and settles Incomplete. A fence-first return event remains absent; commit-first retains exactly one event. Neither may reopen fairness. Capture metadata is distinct from the `return_presented` event and from permission to continue. A timeout, failed read or old callback cannot reduce retained exposure or turn a draft into a saved claim. Positive and zero amounts have the same rule.

If a presentation check fails while the same document/view/owner remains valid, another presentation check may be attempted; **no return write is admissible before confirmed capture**. A definitively aborted return write may be retried by its exact request only while play is still active. After withdrawal, no absent scored return may be retried, even if capture had succeeded; a narrow resolver may reconcile/fence and persist the strongest captured evidence with the Incomplete terminal under the original identity. Until that save is confirmed, label evidence as an unsaved draft. A repeated read failure leaves the fact unknown and scored entry blocked. A failed check caused by invalid display or lost owner withdraws play instead of retrying the same invalid view. A source callback alone cannot prove pixels.

After document termination, a previously persisted `captured_visible` fact remains captured. If successful capture was known only in lost document memory, recovery cannot reconstruct it: a committed transfer with no confirmed return must be treated conservatively as `possibly_revealed`, never inferred `not_revealed` from a missing event. It remains consumed, non-resumable and not a valid complete result. Confirmed deletion removes its attempt and exposure and cannot be reversed by late readback or callback.

The `deletionCases` expand arm/transfer/return/terminal/report/help/history activity × individual/Delete all × deletion-first/subject-first. Individual deletion preserves the counter/tombstone; Delete all replaces installation. Both prevent old writes, activity and DOM publication from resurfacing. The actual current library/history view determines the post-deletion destination; no old callback may steal it. Read and publication occur inside one ordered authority boundary against deletion, including when BroadcastChannel is absent. Failed opens yield an unavailable/retryable view, not a saved claim. No new scored arm is allowed while a previous exact request or settlement remains unresolved. These original local safeguards do not claim to describe the JobTestPrep service.

## 3. Events, validity and report

Version identifiers: `money_exchange_1_spec_v1_0`, `money_exchange_1_forms_v1_0`, `money_exchange_1_event_v1_0`, `money_exchange_1_scoring_v1_0`, `money_exchange_1_storage_v1_0`. Every attempt records installation ID, request/session ID, form ID/digest, form ordinal, device/input/accessibility context, route (standalone or versioned mock Game 10), and ordered event IDs. Award/connection/explanation pages are prearm UI stages, not scored journal events. Only committed actions enter the journal: `form_armed`, `transfer_committed(sentCents)`, `return_presented(returnedCents, final balances)`, `fairness_committed(0..10)` or `fairness_not_applicable` on fairness **Next**, then `completed`; interruption, Restart before completion or technical invalidity uses the alternative terminal `ended_incomplete(reason)`. Rating/N/A and `completed` are committed atomically from the accepted fairness Next; there is no durable rating-only completion claim. Tentative typing, future hidden rates, fairness radio selection before Next and unrelated keys are not performance events. `report_opened` is separate idempotent view activity keyed by a fresh opening ID and cannot alter the game journal.

An outcome is `return_presented` only after the actual result text/graphics are attached to a visible, current, owned document and a browser presentation check succeeds. This is evidence of display, not proof of reading or comprehension. If presentation cannot be confirmed, do not claim a completed valid result or a new unseen form. Before capture, possibly painted pixels require `possibly_revealed`; after confirmed capture, preserve `captured_visible` regardless of later write outcome. Committed transfer, displayed outcome, rating and terminal persistence are distinct facts. A lost callback or navigation cannot invent later facts.

A Valid complete report requires one matching form and accepted arm, one eligible transfer, exact form-derived return and balances, one confirmed positive-transfer rating (or zero-transfer N/A), one completed terminal and no contradiction. Invalid/Incomplete attempts remain reviewable but are not valid baselines. A valid report shows: version/form and repeat label; completion/save status; sent amount, tripled pot, returned amount, final candidate/partner amounts and explicit equations; fairness rating or N/A; device/input/accessibility and interruption context; local replay/history/delete; limitations. An original **How the amounts were calculated** action opens a short arithmetic-only panel and returns to this exact saved report. It supplies no transfer/rating advice and does not copy the saved report's proprietary tips. A descriptive observation may explain the recorded allocation; do not grade the choice or rating or call it trust, morality, personality, employer fitness or a hiring predictor. No Advanced Training exists for this game in Public Beta 0.1. The sums use integer cents and render two decimal places. The filmed `$5/$6.15` yields `$11.15/$8.85` exactly; the partner kept `$8.85` of `$15`.

## 4. Storage, ownership and interruption

Use a versioned local IndexedDB root with installation identity, monotone revision, form counter, retained attempts, form exposure and deletion tombstones. The final pre-decision Next performs one atomic arm transaction comparing current root and request identity, assigning the next form and writing an armed attempt. Repeating the exact request returns the same allocation; conflicting reuse is rejected. The transfer field appears only after authoritative arm readback. A same-browser Web Lock fences concurrent scored ownership; unsupported storage/ownership leaves scored entry unavailable with a recoverable explanation. Prearm Restart never acquires a form.

Every transfer/outcome/rating and terminal commit checks installation, exact attempt, expected revision, tombstone and current owner inside the write order. Visible stage publication, including Test Complete, Next-to-report, help return and Restart, shares an ordered authority boundary with individual deletion and Delete all; a positive read that can go stale before publication is insufficient. The transfer choice is recorded before partner response is published. Save failures, held callbacks and lost deliveries use same-request exact-byte readback/retry; a pending or uncertain commit is never labeled saved, and a stale callback cannot advance the screen or resurrect data. If a result was visible but durable confirmation fails, keep a labeled in-tab uncertain draft or interrupted review; do not expose a new scored attempt as if the form were unseen. Terminal snapshots copy immutable bytes before reentrant listeners run. A bounded recovery path lets history resolve a committed terminal even if its callback never arrives. Restart cancels the current UI epoch before any new prearm stage and then settles the old attempt under its original request ID; a held old save/frame/connection cannot own the new screen. If settlement is unconfirmed, keep the old attempt labeled uncertain and do not allocate a replacement scored form until exact authoritative resolution.

Recovery readback distinguishes a transactionally fenced absent arm (no form), a committed arm (one consumed form), a definitively uncommitted transfer (same edit remains), a committed transfer (no duplicate response), an unconfirmed displayed return (seen/unknown exposure) and an atomic completed terminal (completion preserved). Withdrawal after any committed arm settles its admitted facts as `ended_incomplete(reason)` unless a completed terminal already exists. A pending terminal may retry the exact accepted rating/completion request only while that attempt and view remain authorized; after withdrawal, fence the absent request and settle Incomplete instead. Repeated read failure is `interrupted_review` retryable, not a negative read. Invalid/unsupported root is blocked without reset. Confirmed deletion or installation replacement withdraws old reports and requests. Close may leave the recovery screen, but a document-lifetime settlement owner retains the obligation; library/history labels it and denies a new arm until resolved. History and report/help openings pass through `view_pending`, with `view_unavailable` on failed read; neither state invents a saved result. Deletion passes through `deletion_pending` with exact same-request readback and cannot resurrect an old terminal or activity. These are original storage/recovery rules, not observed source gameplay.

Hide/pagehide, unsupported profile/input changes and owner loss interrupt the current attempt; no mid-game resume. Local history and report openings revalidate current complete root at the point of publication, including without BroadcastChannel; deletion/replacement wins over stale reads and activities. Individual deletion removes its attempt/report activity and adds a tombstone without reducing the exposure counter. Delete all replaces this game's installation and clears only its data. Unsupported/corrupt historical roots are never silently reset or rescored. Saved logical report bytes remain versioned and immutable across later versions.

The full mock may launch this game as Game 10 with an existing versioned mock run ID and accessibility context. Only an authoritatively saved Valid terminal lets the orchestrator advance to Game 11. An interrupted Game 10 restarts this game from its beginning on another allocated form; earlier completed games are preserved. The mock never resumes inside this game. The approved break remains after Game 6. No employer or composite score is created.

## 5. Accessibility, visual acceptance and rights

Native labeled input, buttons and 0–10 radio group support pointer, keyboard, touch and screen-reader operation. Focus follows the next decision/heading, with a concise live announcement of transfer, return and final balances; no rapidly changing announcement or color-only identity. All values retain textual candidate/partner labels. Reduced motion removes nonessential transfer animation without skipping the returned-value stage. Check reflow at 320 px, 200% zoom, high contrast, visible focus and assistive activation. A nonvisual user receives the same arithmetic; actual assistive-technology qualification is a later gate. The layout should preserve the saved wide-screen left/right participant relationship and central text while adapting to narrow viewports. Use original circles/icons, text, colors and composition rather than traced assets, provider logos or copied report language.
