# Assessment Practice — Lengths Specification V1.0

**Status:** agent-authored under the founder's 2026-10-05 full-product delegation in `AGENTS.md`. This draft is **not individually founder-reviewed, independently audited, or frozen for implementation**. Its exact form and fixture identities are below. A separate read-only specification/numerical review must pass before product code begins. This packet governs standalone Practice and the Lengths adapter within the original twelve-game mock; it adds no Advanced Training for Lengths.

## 1. Purpose, provenance, and claim boundary

Lengths is an original visual-comparison exercise: two horizontal bars appear together and the candidate selects the longer one before a fixed deadline. No trial has equal bars. The exercise familiarizes a candidate with simple visual decisions under time pressure. Its report describes only observed choices, omissions, browser-observed times and condition/block counts in Assessment Practice. It is not an employer task replica, psychometric measure, perception diagnosis, or evidence of hiring suitability. Bar geometry, order, text, color treatment and scoring are specified here from first principles. The only assets are generated SVG rectangles, labels and UI typography; no competitor image, copy, logo, normative score or proprietary stimulus is used.

Version identities are `lengths_spec_v1_0`, `lengths_forms_v1`, `lengths_timing_v1`, `lengths_input_v1`, `lengths_event_v1`, `lengths_storage_v1`, `lengths_scoring_v1`, `lengths_activity_v1` and `lengths_mock_adapter_v1`. Any substantive change creates a new version; old attempts retain their recorded versions and are never silently rescored.

## 2. Fixed visual task and forms

Each form has **96 scored trials in four blocks of 24**. On every trial, two centered solid horizontal bars occupy separate equal 240-unit bays in an original `viewBox="0 0 520 100"` SVG: the left bay spans x=0–240, the right x=280–520, and both bars share y=50. The bar is a centered rectangle of the recorded length and the variant's recorded height. Stable visible `LEFT` and `RIGHT` labels identify bays; these labels never indicate the answer. Neither bar is animated. Both are displayed simultaneously and remain visible through the response window. A dark-on-light or light-on-dark palette must meet at least 7:1 bar/background contrast. Color conveys no answer information. The bars and labels are connected, visible and inside the viewport before onset is confirmed; invisible hidden text never supplies a bar length or correct side during timed play.

The **standard** family `LS1`–`LS6` uses short-bar lengths 108, 124, 140 and 156 SVG units, gaps 16 (`small`), 32 (`medium`) and 48 (`large`) units, and 12-unit bar height. The separately labeled **visual accommodation** family `LA1`–`LA6` uses short lengths 96, 112, 128 and 144, gaps 24/40/56 for those same category names, and 20-unit bar height. In either family the long bar is exactly `shortLength + gap`, so the maximum is 204 units, inside its 240-unit bay. The correct response is `longerSide`. Accommodation changes geometry and must appear in the guide, setup, history and report; no LS/LA equivalence or direct improvement claim is made. A profile change after arm invalidates the attempt.

Each block has exactly one of each `gapCategory × longerSide × shortLength` tuple: 8 trials per gap category, 12 with Left longer and 12 with Right longer. A complete form therefore has 32 trials per gap and 48 per correct side. Every block has 8–16 side switches among its 23 internal transitions; across the full order, including block boundaries, no run of a side or gap category exceeds three. These are scheduling constraints, not difficulty calibration.

`scripts/generate-lengths-forms.mjs` is the deterministic constructor for `ASSESSMENT_PRACTICE_LENGTHS_FORMS_V1_0.json`. For form number 1–6 use unsigned seed `0x1e470001 + (number − 1)`. Its xorshift32 step is `state ^= state << 13; state ^= state >>> 17; state ^= state << 5`, with unsigned conversion after the full step. For each block, enumerate cards in category order small/medium/large, side order left/right, base-index order 0–3. Fisher–Yates shuffle from index 23 to 1 using `next() % (index + 1)`; rejected candidate blocks consume their random draws. Accept the first candidate meeting the switch/run constraints with the already accepted prefix, or fail after 100,000 candidates for that block. The two variants map the **same accepted categorical order** to their distinct fixed lengths. There is no live random generator. Across any two numbered orders, at least 70 of 96 `category/side/base-index` positions differ; this is variety, not calibrated equivalence.

The SHA-256 of compact JSON `{version,baseSeed,forms}` is `671b48c20e32daa886ce79e25a6265f4577a5dc069af8d5417328fd1ae0b39e7`. The fixed file includes that digest. The fixture payload `{version,formId,cases}` digest is `84d38be93fa2a53674c1eb2ca0cfa2c40228a9f37a9892e7f12a84ee1794488d`. Verify regeneration and digests before use. A missing, altered or unsupported manifest fails admission rather than substituting stimuli.

## 3. Assignment and candidate journey

Standalone Library → original rule guide → two untimed worked examples (Left longer at a small gap; Right longer at a large gap, with visible answer explanation) → two comprehension questions (which side to choose; when a press counts) → mode/contrast/magnification and input check → readiness → four scored blocks → report/history. Wrong comprehension answers reveal the rule and may be retried without consuming a form. Examples, setup, readiness and between-block summaries are untimed. A candidate may pause at a between-block summary in the same live document without an idle timeout; reload still ends that attempt as incomplete. Each block summary states correct/wrong/omitted counts from its 24 closed trials but no report-level interpretation. Continue starts the next block only after a confirmed checkpoint and fresh visibility/input/profile check. No scoring or correctness feedback appears inside a scored block.

The installation keeps separate durable arm counters for standard and accommodation forms. An atomic accepted arm assigns family form `((counter mod 6) + 1)`, increments only that family's counter once, and stores the complete frozen profile and initial journal. A rejected or uncommitted arm consumes no position. A committed arm remains counted even if fixation never begins. The same start request ID deduplicates retries. Individual deletion does not decrement either counter; Delete all replaces the Lengths installation and resets both. Replay takes the next assigned form. A form may repeat after six accepted arms; the UI never calls a form unseen, new to the candidate or difficulty-equivalent. The mock uses the same family counter chosen by pregame setup, so an interrupted mock attempt consumes its form and a restart uses the next available position; after exhaustion, the repeat is labeled.

## 4. Input contract

Keyboard mode defaults to physical `ArrowLeft` and `ArrowRight` codes. A candidate may assign two distinct codes from `ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Space`, `KeyA`–`KeyZ`, `Digit0`–`Digit9`, `Numpad0`–`Numpad9`, `Comma`, `Period`, `Slash`, `Semicolon`, `Quote`, `BracketLeft`, `BracketRight`, `Backslash`, `Backquote`, `Minus`, `Equal`. Tab, Enter, Escape, navigation/function/system and modifier keys cannot be response bindings. Setup requires a fresh unmodified keydown/keyup check for each side and shows the captured code. During scoring, reject `event.repeat`, composition, Ctrl/Alt/Meta/Shift chords and selected-key presses held across Start, fixation or onset; a keyup must re-arm that key. Prevent selected-key page scrolling only during timed play. Untimed buttons keep native Enter/Space behavior. Unrelated raw keys are neither retained nor scored.

Pointer mode displays large `Left longer` and `Right longer` controls. Only primary `pointerdown` with button 0 and one active pointer ID counts; record mouse, touch or pen source in the frozen profile. A second active pointer is ignored with a quality event. Window-level pointerup/cancel releases a held press even outside the control. A click following pointerdown is never a second answer, and keyboard activation of a pointer-mode control does not become a timed answer. Pointerdown on Start/Continue cannot carry into a trial. The candidate chooses one input mode before arm; its class, codes or pointer source, declared device class, alternative-input declaration, standard/accommodation family, contrast choice, magnification declaration and reduced-motion choice are frozen for all blocks. The measured viewport and browser context are recorded at arm and each Continue but are not claimed to be a physical display measurement; a between-block resize is allowed when the layout predicate still passes. Supported assistive output is admitted only after both directions produce the chosen fresh key or primary pointer pair in setup; click-only/unknown-output methods remain available for untimed learning but cannot start timed play. Alternative-input results are labeled separately. The two modes are not pooled into an unlabeled timing comparison.

For a presented trial, the first fresh selected-side action sampled at `onsetMs ≤ actionMs < onsetMs + 1,400` is the sole scored response. Later selected actions are factual extras and cannot change it. A selected action at exactly 1,400 ms or later, but before confirmed offset, is a boundary extra; if no earlier response, the trial is omitted. Actions before confirmed onset are ignored/recorded as early selected actions. Never use the browser event object's timestamp as the scoring clock.

## 5. Presentation, timing and validity

Use one `performance.now()` monotonic clock and clock-domain ID within a live document; never subtract across reload domains. Start acquires exclusive browser ownership, passes admission and confirms the durable arm by transaction completion **and authoritative readback** before protection begins. At accepted `startMs`, write the fixation immediately. Confirm connected visible fixation at `fixationMs` on the next animation frame; its due time is `startMs` for block-first trials and the prior confirmed `offsetMs` for later trials. Every trial has a **600-ms fixation** from its confirmed fixation time. At `stimulusDueMs = fixationMs + 600`, write both bars/labels together and confirm their visible onset on a frame at `onsetMs ≥ stimulusDueMs`. The response deadline and resolution due time are exactly `onsetMs + 1,400`. A callback before that time waits. At actual `resolutionMs ≥ resolutionDueMs`, resolve omission if needed, request bar removal immediately, and confirm absence at `offsetMs ≥ resolutionMs`. No trailing fixation follows a block's final offset. Nominal complete scored display is 192 seconds plus browser frames and untimed breaks.

Record every stage's due and actual time and `latenessMs = max(0, actual − due)`; offset due time is the original resolution due time, so a late resolution cannot hide offset delay. A stage lateness or foreground protected-frame gap **greater than 250 ms** invalidates. A value **greater than 50 through 250 ms** records a timing note and excludes its affected trial from every time summary; 50 is clean and 250 is non-severe. Fixation lateness affects its following trial, while offset lateness and a protected frame gap affect the current trial. A first response under 100 ms remains an accuracy response but is excluded from time summaries. A separate 2,000-ms watchdog from the original due time of any required fixation, onset, resolution or offset invalidates at its first runnable check at/after that boundary if the stage has not occurred or confirmed; late completion cannot erase the failure. This is not a wall-clock guarantee during OS suspension. Confirmed onset is recorded before any immediate invalidation for that same frame; canceled callbacks cannot invent presentation.

Protection runs from accepted Start/Continue through the block's final confirmed offset or invalidation. Hide, blur, freeze, page exit, lost ownership, input/profile change, layout failure, severe timing failure or unsupported browser behavior during protection invalidates and preserves the draft. Hiding during untimed preparation or between blocks does not itself invalidate; Continue rechecks all admission predicates. A resize/zoom can be accepted between blocks only if the measured layout still passes; a failed protected check invalidates. A stale timer/frame/input callback cannot create a second onset, closure, response or report after invalidation or owner release.

Before Start/Continue and on every protected frame, measure the connected visible fixation or full stimulus footprint, both response controls in pointer mode, and the surrounding layout. They must be inside the visual viewport, unobscured by app overlays and within the horizontal layout viewport without timed-play scrolling. The SVG must render at **at least 520 CSS pixels wide**; if not, disable Start/Continue with a clear resize/lower-zoom message and keep untimed guide/history available. A supported desktop viewport at 200% zoom is tested against this same predicate rather than assumed valid. Do not silently shrink the bars into a different task at narrow widths. A 320-pixel preview tests the explanation and disabled-start state, not timed mobile qualification.

## 6. Event, persistence and recovery contract

The immutable ordered timed journal contains at least `prepared`, `form_assigned`, `armed`, `block_started`, `fixation_confirmed`, `stimulus_presented`, first `response` or `omission`, selected-side early/repeated/extra/boundary actions, selected-key/pointer releases, timing/quality/lifecycle events, `stimulus_offset`, `trial_closed`, `block_summary`, `block_continued`, and exactly one `invalidated` or `session_completed` terminal event when terminal. Every event has schema version, installation/session/form/variant and clock-domain identity, contiguous sequence number and sampled monotonic time; trial events have one-based trial/block identity, and stage events have due/actual/lateness values. A response records chosen side, input class/source, physical code or pointer type and handler time. `armed` and `block_continued` include the frozen full profile. No unrelated raw key, account or employer identity is kept.

Use a separate `assessment-practice-lengths-v1` IndexedDB database with one versioned installation root: installation ID, revision, counters by variant, attempts, variant/ordinal-bearing session tombstones and optional separate `lengths_activity_v1` journal. The retained attempts plus tombstones must reconcile exactly to each variant's counter. A start request includes a unique request/session/clock-domain ID, frozen profile, variant, and route identity: `standalone` or `mock` with mock-run ID, mock version and game index 7. The arm transaction validates current installation/version, assignment and unique request/session IDs, increments one counter and inserts the three-event prefix atomically. Start is accepted only after exact arm/counter readback, including route identity. If delivery is lost, retry/readback with the same request ID; an unavailable readback means Start unconfirmed and no fixation. A cancelable owner acquisition/readback must not strand the lock, and late completion cannot start play. All trial input, resolution and frames operate synchronously without awaiting storage.

At each untimed block break, atomically checkpoint the complete known event prefix, closed-trial count, frozen profile and derived review under the same attempt and revision. Continue requires exact authoritative confirmation; failure leaves an in-tab review-only draft and retry. At invalidation, exit or completion, copy immutable bytes before owner release, then save a terminal snapshot out of timing. A failed/lost completion write is labeled unsaved/uncertain until exact readback or same-byte retry proves it. An armed or checkpoint-only attempt after reload is **incomplete, review-only, with later display/exposure unknown**; no timed block or break resumes. A new arm restarts at Block 1. Do not treat unpersisted post-checkpoint events as known omissions.

On opening a verified saved report/review, generate one random view ID for that opening and append an idempotent `report_viewed` activity event with next activity sequence, installation/session identity, source surface and wall-clock observation. Retry uses the same ID. Activity neither changes timed event bytes nor enters response-time arithmetic. An unsupported historic schema remains non-destructively review-only until an explicit versioned migration; no silent rescoring. Validate manifest, version, identity, event order, feasible display/input, summary bytes, profile and deletion authority before a saved claim.

Individual deletion atomically removes an attempt, its report and activity and writes a session tombstone; counters remain. Delete all atomically replaces the Lengths installation and resets both counters, attempts, activity and tombstones without touching other games. Every delayed arm/checkpoint/terminal/report-view transaction checks current installation and tombstone authority before writing. After deletion, active UI withdraws the report before displaying saved data again; cross-tab notifications are advisory, while opening/resuming always rereads authoritative storage.

## 7. Reconciliation, scoring and report

A Valid run has exactly 96 manifest-matched presented and closed trials in order, one first eligible response or omission per trial, four block boundaries, a `session_completed` event and no invalidation. Saved/unsaved/uncertain is a separate storage claim requiring exact terminal readback for `saved`. Define `presented = closed + unresolvedPresented` and `96 = presented + unpresented` for every status. An invalid event yields Invalid even if all 96 closed. A missing terminal event after crash/reload yields Incomplete. An observed response on an unresolved trial is not classified correct/wrong; an unpresented or unresolved trial is never called omitted. Invalid and Incomplete reviews show status/reason, factual presented/closed/unresolved/unpresented counts and ordered evidence, but suppress all aggregate accuracy percentages, per-category tables, medians, IQRs and contrasts.

On Valid runs, `correct + wrong + omitted = 96`; a response is correct iff its selected side equals the manifest's `longerSide`. Show overall correct/wrong/omitted with denominator 96, each gap category with denominator 32, and each block with denominator 24. Show response tendency as factual Left and Right selected counts among responded trials, denominator `responded = correct + wrong`, beside the balanced 48/48 correct-side opportunities. Zero responded displays “No response-side distribution,” not 0%. A practical observation may describe the largest within-run count difference by category or side, using exact counts; it must not infer a stable bias, trait or improvement. Do not compare standard and accommodation forms as equivalent.

For Valid runs only, a clean correct response time is `firstEligibleActionMs − confirmedOnsetMs` when `100 ≤ RT < 1,400`, no affected >50-ms timing note/frame gap exists, and input/profile identity matches the accepted arm. Show median clean correct RT overall, by gap category and by block only with at least five values in that cell. With 5–7 values show the median and “Not enough clean responses for variability”; with at least eight show median-of-halves IQR. For odd `n`, exclude the central observation from both halves; for even `n`, each median is the mean of the two central values. Fewer than five shows “Not enough clean responses for a time summary.” Sort raw times, compute before display rounding; round displayed milliseconds half away from zero and count percentages half-up to one decimal. Never fabricate a zero median for an empty cell. A category-time contrast is deliberately omitted in V1.0 because different gaps are not calibrated difficulty levels.

Every report shows validity and saved/unsaved/uncertain state; recorded form, geometry variant and versions; frozen device/input/accessibility context; interruption reason or “none recorded”; supported counts and time summaries; Replay; local-history/deletion controls; original-form and physical latency limits; and “Advanced Training is not available for Lengths in this beta.” It cannot display employer scores, pass thresholds, personality conclusions, percentiles or guaranteed improvement. History labels standard/accommodation, alternative input, repeated form, invalid and incomplete attempts separately.

## 8. Accessibility and mock behavior

Provide high contrast, scalable SVG, the 520-CSS-pixel measured minimum, visible focus, non-color `LEFT`/`RIGHT` side cues, large pointer targets, reduced motion, keyboard route through preparation/report/history and a separately labeled visual accommodation family. Any extra browser magnification is allowed only when the measured stimulus and controls fit. Focus moves to untimed headings and actions; the timed comparison announces only a neutral “Compare the bars” cue. Do not expose exact lengths or the correct side through speech/DOM metadata during timing. State before scored Start that visual comparison is not currently supported through spoken bar interpretation; a candidate who declares that need cannot begin timed Lengths in V1.0 but retains the untimed guide and other games. No nonvisual equivalence is claimed.

In the original twelve-game mock, Lengths is Game 7, after the optional midpoint break. Pregame settings select the family; the mock's saved identity and the Lengths arm bind each other. A valid confirmed terminal result advances the mock only after authoritative readback. An interrupted Lengths game is retained Invalid/Incomplete for review, never resumed within the game; the mock resumes at the between-game boundary and restarts Lengths with the next family form where available, labeling repeats after exhaustion. Mock resumed/multi-session status, accommodation breaks and summary are governed by the Master Strategy. No Lengths-specific Advanced Training or mock composite score is added.

## 9. Deterministic acceptance fixtures and implementation gates

These expectations are required, not executable product logic. In particular, eight values 100–800 ms have median 450, Q1 250, Q3 650 and IQR 400; five values 100–500 have median 300 but no IQR. Additional implementation fixtures must cover a response held through onset, modifier/repeat rejection, two pointer contacts, canceled/late frames, 50/250/251-ms frame gaps, missing-stage watchdog, block checkpoint failure/retry, completion write lost delivery, unchanged timed bytes after report view, same-ID activity retry, deletion/replacement fences, two-tab ownership, reload review and mock restart. Expected counts must be derived from primitive manifest/action records rather than copied from a stored `expected` field.
