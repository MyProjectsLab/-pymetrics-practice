## Blocking findings

**B1 — Individual result deletion does not delete the result data.** On a completed ME2-1 attempt, Local history → Delete A1.1 invokes `deleteAttempt` (`entry.ts:39-44`). The reducer removes only `root.attempts[id]` and `root.activity[id]`, leaving `root.outcomes` untouched (`core.ts:95-104`). Every committed command is stored there in full (`core.ts:19-22`), including the exact `choice_1` and `choice_2` signed cents and `fairness_1`/`fairness_2` ratings. The retained production-browser `evidence/filmed/filmed-root.json` has 24 outcomes, including two `cents:200` choice commands and two `rating:8` rating/terminal commands. Applying the implementation's delete branch removes the history attempt while leaving those four personal play values in the same IndexedDB root. The screen promises “Delete this result? Form familiarity will remain,” and history says only form familiarity remains. The deletion browser harness checks attempt removal but likewise preserves `outcomes` (`browser-deletion.mjs:5-8`); its PASS does not cover these bytes. `validateRoot` replays the complete ledger (`core.ts:269-287`), so clearing entries naively would break validation. Preserve the needed form-exposure/tombstone facts without retaining deleted per-attempt choice/rating/report details, then test complete root bytes after individual deletion, reload, same-request readback, and deletion races. Delete all separately resets the ledger and is not this finding.

**B2 — Source-size choice screen misses the frozen visual structure.** Compare the retained 2668×1704 candidate `evidence/matrix/source-size-choice.png` with the saved source frame `/private/tmp/money2-frames/075.png` (filmed first-round $1.50 preview), under source record V01/V02 and specification §5. The filmed decision has a near-full-height white central panel, wide-separated people, a large central green amount tile above a green horizontal choice bar with endpoint labels, Restart at the upper right, and the purple advancement control at the lower right. Candidate instead has a substantially shorter white panel with large gray empty area below, a small plain-text amount and native thin range near the top disconnected from the participant row, and Confirm/Restart/Close grouped at the lower left. The required central choice bar/tile and navigation hierarchy are missing; color and original glyphs alone do not meet the packet's relative composition requirement. The native slider may be kept for accessibility, but its visual treatment and control placement need to preserve the approved hierarchy. This is a visual-source gate failure, not a numerical or money-flow failure.
