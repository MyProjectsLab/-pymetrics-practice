## Why category denominators matter

Identical fixed behavior alone changes overall accuracy across these mixes. Always responding scores 28/80 = **35%** on a 28-GO form and 45/80 = **56.25%** on a 45-GO form. Never responding scores 52/80 = **65%** and 35/80 = **43.75%**, respectively. Each direction spans 21.25 percentage points with no behavioral change. Consequently, different-mix overall-accuracy deltas must be suppressed; report GO and NO-GO rates with their actual denominators. Same-mix forms remain uncalibrated and may vary by sequence context or ordinary performance.

## Limits and pending review

The packet deliberately includes only 14 fixed original sequences. It does not establish matched difficulty, norm scores, a real-Pymetrics distribution, or that 28/52 and 45/35 are typical rather than two observed JobTestPrep practice runs. A separate reviewer must independently regenerate the seeds and challenge the scoring, routing, old-chain migration and comparison contracts before implementation begins.
