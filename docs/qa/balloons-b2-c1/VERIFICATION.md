## Behavior implemented

- A reservation transaction rechecks the current durable, valid BL and the first valid saved completion of its assigned drill in an open chain. It selects the lowest-numbered RT that has no confirmed or uncertain exposure, independent of the BL number. It records the actual BL and RT identities, original chain mapping, compatible settings and 30-minute issue/expiry wall times. A new owner releases any prior unarmed RT reservation before opening another; only a browser-lock-backed owner can reserve or act.
- No scored element mounts until a current-owner transaction durably arms the presentation. Two visible animation frames with a nonzero scored element and category/controls confirm onset; Pump and Collect remain disabled until the `form_exposed` write is authoritative. An unarmed exit or pre-arm expiry releases the RT. A possible mount, aborted exposure write or interruption retains uncertainty. Post-arm expiry plus confirmed onset consumes and invalidates the exact RT; expiry after a confirmed onset cannot release it. Confirmed onset evidence survives a canceled delivery path when it can be committed under the same still-current owner.
- Retest actions, 39 round results, interruption and recovery use the existing immutable Balloons journal and fixed RT manifests. Recovery preserves the same session, mapping, settings and committed action prefix. Failed or uncertain writes require reconciliation before saved copy or eligibility. An exposed invalid attempt can resume as itself or request a fresh attempt with the next unseen RT. Tombstones preserve used-form exposure; deleting an unarmed RT leaves a released-unseen tombstone. Only one valid, compatible, durable RT completion closes the chain. Exhausted RT inventory cannot close it.
- The local report identifies both actual form IDs and states that BL–RT comparison is not yet available. The training and history routes expose explanation, resume and valid fresh-attempt controls without adding comparison claims.

## Development corrections and limits

The first full check exposed a real deletion-boundary error: the existing router treated any same-chain result tombstone as deletion of the baseline. Its baseline-chain deletion check now applies to BL tombstones, while RT tombstones continue to preserve exposure inventory. The corrected focused and full checks pass. Earlier browser probes also exposed a training-to-retest owner-lock handoff race and the missing drill-manifest load on reload; both were corrected before the final run. Preliminary probe failures caused by driver labels/focus and premature IndexedDB reads were corrected in the driver and are not counted as product passes. The external directory retains preliminary failed snapshots and logs separately from the final summaries.

Automated native button activation does not certify physical keyboard timing, screen-reader speech, display scanout or the approved browser/device/accessibility matrix. Injected blur/hidden, failed writes and held callbacks are bounded race evidence. This is a developer-tested C1 handoff, not an independent audit, whole-B1/mock or full-B2 verdict, founder acceptance, qualification, deployment or release. N1 and OBS-1 remain open on their prior terms.
