## Delivered design

- `authority.ts` owns stable original-arm families and immutable result children, including separate identities for different complete snapshot bytes. Operation ports freeze store identity/generation, request/arm/result, operation ID/kind, and initiating navigation/opening token. Cross-store, cross-arm, cross-result and cross-surface ports are rejected. Store connections in one native database/backend domain share original-installation authority handles, while each operation retains its originating Store identity. Exact-target reattachment reuses absorbing retirement.
- Entry and Store emit typed installation, arm and result observations at verified transaction/read boundaries, including prefix compatibility and its captured verified revision floor. The underlying verified read delivers observations before `withAbort` can mask completion; fallback catches cannot discard evidence. Start retries and individual deletion capture existing exact arm scopes at dispatch. Committed deletion/replacement readback retires the original scope before UI freshness checks. Corrupt/unavailable data and forbidden/revision-conflicting writes do not manufacture deletion.
- Recovery uses that authority handle from admission onward. Generic Promise-error retirement was removed. Confirmed snapshots bind immutable result identity; retained prefix compatibility remains separate from exact saved publication. Existing recovery coalescing/deadlines, cancellation and ordered read-to-DOM publication remain.
- Journey attaches live, released and history reports through one subscriber. Retirement synchronously clears all result sources, counts/events, saved markers and retries. History remains read-only, including absent-snapshot rows. Activity captures the original store/handle/opening and retries the same immutable opening; its 2-second delivery deadline cannot block the surface. Late success/failure only affects its own attached opening. Initial store opening and Delete all adoption/navigation are generation-fenced.
