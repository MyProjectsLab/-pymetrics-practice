Privacy and usage counts

Your practice records

Detailed answers, response times, journals and saved reports stay in this browser when local storage is available. There is no participant account or cross-device sync. You can delete these local records from each assessment’s history. Temporary records disappear on close or reload.

What we collect

We count visits and practice-link clicks on information pages. We also collect one limited summary when a fresh supported assessment reaches a confirmed ending: its task, version, practice or mock context, and completion status. Completed attempts may include totals such as correct answers, Balloons pumps and banked practice money, Cards balance, or Towers moves. Money Exchange sends completion counts only. We do not send individual answers, response times, journals, local identifiers, accommodation choices, names or email addresses.

Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable collection. Task summaries start sending only after timed work and local confirmation end. They never control a score or delay saving. Leaving, hiding or closing the page cancels pending task collection; a request already transmitted cannot be recalled. Existing information-page click requests may finish after leaving.

What the counts mean

These are recorded attempts, not unique people or employer benchmarks. Closing, reloading, privacy settings, blockers, unsupported modes and connection failures leave gaps. Saved reports are not uploaded again. We cannot count every abandoned start. Repeated practice, task versions and devices can affect the results.

Provider and retention

The provider is Umami Cloud. Requests go directly to its collection service. We use no analytics cookies, browser storage, custom visitor IDs, session replay or heatmaps.

Umami receives the request IP address and browser information as part of normal network delivery. It derives approximate location and estimates visitor groups using the site, IP address, browser information and a salt that rotates monthly by default. It says the IP is not a field in standard analytics records; infrastructure and security logs are separate. This is not a promise of absolute anonymity.

The free Hobby plan retains production analytics for six months. Backup and operational retention are governed separately by the provider’s terms; six months is not a guaranteed deletion deadline for every copy. Deleting practice history removes local records, not summaries already received by Umami. We send no local attempt ID that could locate an earlier summary. Counts are approximate: privacy settings, blockers, connectivity and navigation may prevent events from arriving. A practice-link click is not a completed assessment.

Provider details: visitor identification and IP processing, Cloud retention, regions and backups, privacy policy.

Hosting and Google Search

Vercel receives ordinary page requests, including their IP addresses and URLs, to serve the site. Some full-mock links include opaque local mock identifiers and slot numbers in the request URL. We do not send practice answers or reports in those requests. Hosting logs have their own provider policies.

Public information pages may appear in search engines. Game, mock and report pages are marked not to be indexed. Search Console reports aggregate Google search impressions, clicks and queries; it does not give us the names of individual searchers.
